## Sources fetched per game

### A Way Out
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Way_Out — Hazelight / EA; March 23 2018; UE4; platforms PS4/XBO/PC; 2-player mandatory; 13M sales by April 2026; BAFTA Multiplayer 2019; Metacritic 78-79
- https://pixelation.org/a-way-out-why-hazelights-co-op-experiment-still-hits-different-years-later-3ch — dynamic split-screen framing detail; asynchronous co-op moment design; Friend's Pass "one purchase, two play" framing; netcode described as "surprisingly stable"; UE4 team of ~30-35; controller-first design; mocap performed by Josef Fares personally
- https://gamingcypher.com/ea-hazelight-announce-friends-pass-free-trial-co-op-adventure-way/ — Friend Trial announced TGA 2017; free download for non-owning partner; full game access; online invite flow (non-owner prompted to download trial from invite)
- https://www.newsweek.com/way-out-friend-pass-guide-how-use-free-full-game-trial-858615 — Friend Pass guide: no matchmaking; friend-invite only; confirmed no random pairing; platform-siloed (no cross-platform crossplay confirmed)
- https://www.gameverdict.net/hazelight-studios-games-revolutionizing-co-op-storytelling-in-modern-gaming/ — Hazelight studio identity; Josef Fares quote on designing for two from the start; no solo option as creative choice

### Overcooked 2
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overcooked_2 — Ghost Town Games / Team17; August 7 2018; Unity engine; 1-4 players; online multiplayer added (Team17 contributed ~15 additional devs); ingredient throwing as new mechanic; Metacritic 81-83; TGA 2018 Best Family Game; 87% OpenCritic recommend
- https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/game-design-deep-dive-building-truly-cooperative-play-in-i-overcooked-i- — Phil Duncan designer postmortem: task asymmetry as core mechanism (more actions than players); time pressure as communication catalyst; design-backward-from-experience methodology per level; lives-system replaced with timed-score (psychological reframe from failure to performance); iconographic UI to reduce communication breakdowns; visual timers per dish/order
- https://www.superjumpmagazine.com/overcooked-how-design-creates-teamwork/ — physical barrier design forcing pass-not-carry; dynamic environment mid-level (tilting ship, earthquake); ingredient timers enabling prioritization; 3-star scoring rewards coordination without hard-gating casual groups; "pinch points" design for routing cooperation
- https://medium.com/game-design-fundamentals/the-mda-of-overcooked-2-f1e3343de8a7 — MDA analysis: fellowship as dominant aesthetic; challenge and sense-pleasure secondary; real-time communication as core game dynamic; accessible for varied skill levels; sessions 30-60 min; levels 3-4 min
- https://ghosttowngames.com/game/overcooked-2/ — studio page; BAFTA-nominated; confirmed online and local co-op; each online player must own the game

### Gang Beasts
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gang_Beasts — Boneloaf (James + Michael Brown); Double Fine Presents publisher (2017-2020), self-published from 2020, Skybound physical retail; Unity engine; PC/PS4 Dec 12 2017, Xbox One Mar 2019, Switch Oct 2021; 22 stages in "Beef City"; modes: Melee, Waves (co-op vs AI), Gang, Soccer; Metacritic 67-68; BAFTA nomination; SXSW nomination
- https://steamcommunity.com/app/285900/discussions/0/483368526577841388/ — Steam community thread confirming online session matching; developers monitored and patched online services post-launch; online support not present at Early Access launch; session matching historically unstable
- https://www.punogames.com/blog/gang-beasts-game-review/ — physics-based ragdoll as primary mechanic; no health bars (environmental elimination); controls deliberately simple (triggers for arms, face buttons for grab/headbutt/kick/jump); stage hazard design; up to 8 players
- https://thatvideogameblog.com/couch-co-op-gang-beasts-pc/ — co-op Waves mode detail; local co-op setup; local vs online quality gap observed
- https://www.co-optimus.com/game/4867/pc/gang-beasts.html — co-op mode confirmed; up to 8 players; online and local; no persistent progression confirmed
- Community mod GBSU (Codeberg mirror) — LAN/online custom play mod exists, suggesting official online layer has gaps

### Barotrauma
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barotrauma_(video_game) — Undertow Games + FakeFish / Daedalic; MonoGame engine; Early Access June 2019; full release March 13 2023; Windows/macOS/Linux; up to 16 players; 6 crew roles; game modes: campaign (Eye of Europa), ship-to-ship, outpost, sandbox; lead dev Joonas "Regalis" Rikkonen (SCP Containment Breach)
- https://gamerant.com/barotrauma-all-crew-roles-explained/ — full 6-role breakdown: Captain (navigation/orders/supply), Engineer (reactor/power/electrical), Mechanic (hull/piping/fabrication), Medical Doctor (health/pharmacology/genetics), Security Officer (firearms/turrets/internal threats), Assistant (flexible/expendable support); role interdependency analysis: Mechanic+Engineer share fabricator; Doctor needs Security protection; Captain depends on all roles
- https://geekflare.com/hosting/best-barotrauma-server-hosting-options/ — P2P and dedicated server hosting both supported; third-party dedicated server hosting available (Shockbyte, GTXGaming, etc.); high-frequency CPU required for physics simulation; dedicated server preferred community model for 16-player sessions; P2P viable for small groups
- https://steamcommunity.com/app/602960 — Steam page confirming single-player (AI crew) and multiplayer modes; traitor mechanic as opt-in; mod ecosystem on Steam Workshop
- https://www.co-optimus.com/game/5538/pc/barotrauma.html — co-op confirmed; up to 16 players; online and LAN supported

### Full Metal Furies
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Metal_Furies — Cellar Door Games (Rogue Legacy studio); Jan 17 2018 PC+XBO; Nov 6 2018 Switch; Metacritic 85 PC / 75 XBO+Switch; "failed to break even" — developer described as "a pretty massive failure" commercially; 3 years development; self-published
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/416600/Full_Metal_Furies/ — 1-4 players; online and local co-op ("full couch and on-line co-op supported, and every variation in between"); solo via Pick 2 quick-switch mechanic; Xbox Play Anywhere + Windows 10 cross-play; 4 characters: Alex (fighter/combos), Meg (sniper/mines), Triss (tank/shield), Erin (engineer/turret); skill trees per character; 4 gear slots per character; SDL_GameController support on macOS/Linux
- https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/switch-eshop/full_metal_furies — barrier system detail: color-coded shields per Fury; only matching Fury can break shield; revival dependency (must physically reach and hold revive); gear leveling benefits whole team; meta-puzzle overlay on combat zones requiring collective decoding; party composition as tactical variable
- https://www.pcgamer.com/rogue-legacy-devs-announce-full-metal-furies-a-four-player-co-op-brawler/ — announcement coverage; RPG skill trees + collectable equipment + secret world meta-puzzles as genre modernization; "barrier system" named as core co-op design
- https://www.cellardoorgames.com/our-games/full-metal-furies — studio page; confirmed 4 Furies; co-op positioning; Steam "Very Positive" 90% of ~989 reviews

## Notes on source quality

- A Way Out: Friend Trial / Friend Pass mechanism confirmed by multiple sources (EA announcement, Newsweek guide, Wikipedia). Netcode architecture not officially documented; "surprisingly stable" is a press/community characterization, not a technical claim. No crossplay confirmed (platform-siloed).
- Overcooked 2: Design deep-dive by Phil Duncan (named designer) is the strongest source in this batch — primary postmortem confirming design methodology. Online netcode architecture not documented; Unity engine confirmed. Team17's 15-dev contribution to online implementation confirmed by Wikipedia.
- Gang Beasts: Physics engine and Unity confirmed. Online architecture is inferred from community sources and mod activity; no official technical documentation. BAFTA/SXSW nominations confirmed. Metacritic scores confirmed.
- Barotrauma: MonoGame engine confirmed by Wikipedia. 16-player cap confirmed. Role breakdown from Game Rant (comprehensive, named roles). Dedicated server support confirmed by third-party hosting market existence and community preference. Traitor mechanic confirmed via Steam page.
- Full Metal Furies: Barrier system confirmed by multiple reviewer sources (Nintendo Life, PCGamer announcement). Commercial failure confirmed by developer (Cellar Door quoted). Metacritic scores confirmed. Online netcode architecture not documented; Steam relay/P2P inferred. "Failed to break even" is a developer self-assessment, not an audited figure.
