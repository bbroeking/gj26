# Raw Research: RPG Batch 4 — Foundational & Indie CRPGs
*Ingested: 2026-06-14*

Six games researched for wiki/games/rpg/. Sources fetched via WebSearch + WebFetch.

## Ultima IV (1985, Origin Systems)
- Virtue system: eight virtues derived from Truth/Love/Courage, tracked by player actions (giving gold, letting enemies flee, honest dialogue). No villain — Avatarhood is the goal.
- Character class assigned from ethical vignette answers at creation.
- First Ultima with interactive NPC keyword dialogue.
- Eight virtue-aligned companions.
- Shrine meditation + Abyss final exam structure.
- Sold 400,000+ copies by 1989. CGW 2nd-best game of all time (1996).
- Garriott shifted design after letters from concerned parents about violence/theft themes.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_IV:_Quest_of_the_Avatar; wiki.ultimacodex.com/wiki/Ultima_IV:_Quest_of_the_Avatar

## Ultima VII: The Black Gate (1992, Origin Systems)
- NPCs with daily schedules (sleep, eat, work, attend cult meetings).
- Nearly every object interactive: bake bread, forge swords, weave cloth, paint portraits.
- First fully mouse-driven Ultima; "gumps" inventory system.
- Fellowship cult mystery solved by observation and dialogue, not combat.
- Real-time combat with party AI.
- Custom DOS extender "Voodoo" — incompatible with Windows 95+; Exult fan engine restored playability.
- $1M development cost. PC Gamer #10 all-time 2011. Garriott's "most masterfully executed" Ultima.
- World-first, story-second production methodology.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_VII:_The_Black_Gate; ultima.fandom.com/wiki/Ultima_VII:_The_Black_Gate; crpgaddict.blogspot.com/2020/03/game-363-ultima-vii-black-gate.html

## Pathfinder: Wrath of the Righteous (2021, Owlcat Games)
- Mythic Paths: Angel, Demon, Lich, Azata, Aeon, Trickster, Gold Dragon, Swarm-That-Walks. Each changes narrative, dialogue, and endings.
- 25+ classes, hundreds of archetypes — full Pathfinder 1e ruleset.
- Crusade layer: turn-based strategy (HoMM-inspired) commanding armies vs demons in the Worldwound. Toggleable to auto.
- Companion approval/disapproval + path-specific reactions; some companions leave based on mythic choice.
- Metacritic 83/100 PC. 1M copies sold by Jan 2023.
- Improved on Kingmaker (2018): fewer bugs, better campaign pacing.
- Kickstarter funded; confirmed market for high-complexity tabletop CRPGs.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pathfinder:_Wrath_of_the_Righteous; techraptor.net/gaming/previews/owlcat-games-goes-mythic-in-pathfinder-wrath-of-righteous

## Shadowrun Returns (2013, Harebrained Schemes)
- Karma: classless spend across any skill/attribute; archetypes pre-spend opening Karma only.
- Six archetypes: Street Samurai, Mage, Shaman, Decker, Rigger, Physical Adept.
- Decker fights a parallel Matrix battle during runs (tactical sub-game).
- Shaman: healing/summoning via animal totems (Rat, Wolf, Eagle spirits).
- Turn-based AP combat (2 AP/turn); authored set-piece encounters.
- Linear narrative: Seattle murder mystery → insect spirit conspiracy.
- Unity engine; $1.89M Kickstarter (met $400K goal in 28 hours). Metacritic 76/100 PC.
- Save-system flaw: saves only at area transitions. Sequels: Dragonfall (2014), Hong Kong (2015).
- Jordan Weisman (original tabletop creator) co-founded Harebrained Schemes.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shadowrun_Returns; pcgamer.com/shadowrun-returns-character-archetypes/; shadowrunreturns.neoseeker.com/wiki/Archetypes

## Wasteland 3 (2020, inXile Entertainment)
- Full 2-player co-op campaign: separate squads, shared companion pool, can split or travel together.
- Async sessions: either player can continue solo when partner is offline.
- Skill-check synergy: game evaluates highest relevant skill across both squads — rewards complementary builds.
- Faction reputation: 5-step scale (Hated → Loved) affecting quest access, encounters, story.
- Can turn in quests to rival factions simultaneously — double reward or mutually exclusive consequences.
- Core moral tension: Patriarch patron vs rebel factions; multiple campaign endings.
- Vehicle (Kodiak) for world-map traversal; environmental hazard gates.
- Unity engine. Metacritic 85/100 PC. 2M players by Dec 2021. Nominated Best RPG TGA 2020.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasteland_3; co-optimus.com/game/4370/pc/wasteland-3.html; wasteland.fandom.com/wiki/Reputation

## Torment: Tides of Numenera (2017, inXile Entertainment)
- Tides: five-color alignment (Blue=wisdom, Red=passion, Indigo=justice, Gold=sacrifice, Silver=power). Dominant Tide shapes Legacy and NPC social reactions. Not good/evil.
- Crisis system: turn-based mode where combat, dialogue, item use, and environmental manipulation are all valid resolution paths. Violence is never the only option.
- Narrative character creation: questions like "how did you survive your first death?" allocate starting stats. Compound identity: "I am a [descriptor] [class] who [focus]."
- Three classes: Glaive, Nano, Jack.
- Setting: Earth 1 billion years future; "numenera" = misunderstood ancient tech that looks like magic. Rewards curiosity + Lore skills.
- Central question: "What does one life matter?" (Player = Last Castoff of the immortal Changing God.)
- Combat mode chosen by backer fan vote during development — turn-based won.
- $4.19M Kickstarter 2013 (then highest ever for a video game). Released Feb 2017. Metacritic 81/100 PC.
- Sources: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torment:_Tides_of_Numenera; torment.fandom.com/wiki/Tides; engadget.com/2013-11-09-torment-tides-of-numenera-update-details-alignment-system-back.html
