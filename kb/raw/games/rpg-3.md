## Key design concepts extracted

- **Hazard channels** (Prey/Mooncrash) — overlay environmental states on fixed geometry for replayability without proc-gen
- **Chaos system** (Dishonored) — world-reactive consequence without explicit moral labeling
- **Design around exploits** (Dishonored) — treat emergent interactions as features; redesign levels to accommodate
- **Gestural spell-casting** (Arx Fatalis) — magic as physical craft skill, not cooldown press
- **Dungeon ecology** (Arx Fatalis) — factions with schedules inhabiting distinct biome levels
- **Skill-by-doing + no level-scaling** (Kenshi) — growth feels earned when the world stays dangerous
- **Conquest prologue** (Tyranny) — backstory as pre-game decision mechanic that writes world state
- **Dual reputation (Favor/Wrath)** (Tyranny) — reputation as multi-dimensional relationship style, not single approval axis
- **Modular spell construction** (Tyranny) — elemental core + expression accent = player-authored abilities
