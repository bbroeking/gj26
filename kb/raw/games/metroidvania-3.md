**Key facts extracted:**
- Released September 4, 2025 on PC/console (not a stub — game is fully shipped as of research date 2026-06-14; Sea of Sorrow DLC incoming 2026)
- Protagonist: Hornet; uses needle, silk, and Tools rather than nail/soul
- Four tool categories: Red (active, limited use, Shell Shards replenish), Blue (passive defensive), Yellow (passive offensive/exploration), White (Silk Skills, spend Silk)
- Crafting Kits permanently upgrade tool damage; Quill auto-updates map at benches
- ~40 legendary bosses, ~200 enemies
- Crest slots gate tool loadout (analogous to Hollow Knight Notch/Charm system)
- Act 3 restricts certain tools; Cling Grip + Faydown Cloak combo unlocks specific zones
- Development: 3-person core team; Pellen described 2–3 year window feeling "12 months away"; studio went silent 2019–2025 by design
- Engine: presumed Unity (same as Hollow Knight) — not explicitly confirmed in sources

## Iconoclasts

**Sources fetched:**

- https://www.gameanim.com/2018/04/24/iconoclasts-animation-and-game-design-details/ (search overview)

- https://students.igda.org/2018/02/16/ama-15-joakim-konjak-sandberg-indie-developer/ (timeout)

**Key facts extracted:**
- Developer: Joakim "Konjak" Sandberg, solo; published by Bifrost Entertainment
- Engine: Construct Classic (Construct 1) — confirmed by Sandberg directly on Twitter/X
- Development: concept ~2007–2008, full-time 2010, shipped January 2018 (~7–10 years)
- Wrench: melee weapon + bolt-turning (puzzles) + grapple + electrify — single multi-role tool
- Auto-aim: 90-degree cone, directional activation, no cursor precision required
- Hitbox-animation decoupling: animation plays longer than mechanical hitbox; attack box on weapon, body box = vulnerable rectangle
- 20+ boss encounters; each applies mechanics from preceding level puzzles
- Redundant attack phases deliberately removed once player has learned the pattern
- Inspiration: art from Monster World 4 (Mega Drive); gameplay from Metroid Fusion

## Shantae Half-Genie Hero

**Sources fetched:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shantae:_Half-Genie_Hero (fetched successfully)
- https://worthplaying.com/article/2016/12/28/reviews/101823 (search overview)
- https://steamcommunity.com/app/253840/discussions/0/154644349170751523/ (search overview)
- https://www.metacritic.com/game/shantae-half-genie-hero/ (search result)

**Key facts extracted:**
- Developer/publisher: WayForward Technologies; released December 20, 2016
- Kickstarter: ~$750 k–$900 k raised (slight discrepancy between sources; $776 k on campaign, rounded variously)
- Engine: WayForward's proprietary engine; 2D vector sprites over 3D backgrounds (HD upgrade from pixel-art tradition)
- Transformation system: Monkey (climb), Elephant (smash), Bat (aerial), Crab (underwater), Mouse (tight spaces), Mermaid, Tank — each new form unlocks area categories
- Stage-based structure: controversial departure from series' open-world metroidvania; revisit stages with new transformations for 100%
- Hair-whip primary attack; magic meter governs spells (no longer single-use items)
- Post-launch DLC modes reuse same stages from different protagonist kits (Friends to the End, Pirate Queen's Quest)
- Matt Bozon styled title "½ Genie Hero" as tribute to Rumiko Takahashi's Ranma ½
- Metacritic: mixed-positive reception; praised visuals/music, criticised content shallowness

## Aeterna Noctis

**Sources fetched:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeterna_Noctis (fetched; limited mechanical detail in Wikipedia)
- https://screenrant.com/aeterna-noctis-game-review/ (fetched successfully)
- https://www.nookgaming.com/aeterna-noctis-review/ (search overview)
- https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/Aeterna_Noctis (cited for engine: Unity)
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/1517970/Aeterna_Noctis/ (search overview)

**Key facts extracted:**
- Developer: Aeternum Game Studios, Talavera de la Reina, Spain
- Engine: Unity
- Released: December 2021; platforms: PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Nintendo Switch, PC
- 16 interconnected zones; ~20-hour campaign
- Protagonist: King of Darkness; regains abilities throughout
- Bonfire equivalent: Throne — resting respawns enemies in surrounding (not immediate) area
- Death mechanic: drops items, one reclaim attempt (Dark Souls bloodstain)
- Two difficulty modes: standard action + "Noctis Mode" (adds precision platforming difficulty layer)
- Skill tree: first-tier upgrades = combat stats (damage, crit); reviewers note lack of depth
- Fast travel: B-69, narrative-flavored space-warping train
- Level design built for movement exploitation / multiple routes
- Metacritic: 67–72 across platforms; OpenCritic 50% recommend
- Aesthetic: Castlevania-adjacent; blends both genre pillars "quite well" despite execution stumbles
- Combat criticism: timing mismatch (axe enemies faster than player sword); platforming: large hitboxes on obstacles

## Prince of Persia The Lost Crown

**Sources fetched:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_of_Persia:_The_Lost_Crown (search overview)

- https://game8.co/games/Prince-of-Persia-The-Lost-Crown/archives/439272 (via search)

**Key facts extracted:**
- Developer: Ubisoft Montpellier (Rayman studio); released January 18, 2024
- Engine: Ubisoft internal proprietary engine (NOT Unity or Unreal; Unreal Engine 5 is for next project)
- Development duration: ~3.5 years
- Protagonist: Sargon, youngest of the Immortals warriors
- 6 Time Powers (total, across campaign): traversal + combat double-duty
  - Rush of Simurgh: air dash; stops mid-air fall after being hit
  - Shadow of Simurgh: place temporal mark → teleport back → carry stored strike
  - Dimensional trap: capture enemy or projectile in another dimension; deploy as temporary ally
- Amulets: charm-like equippables (Hollow Knight parallel explicitly noted in reviews); combat upgrades, no area unlocks; found via exploration, boss drops, or Time Crystal purchase
- Athra Abilities: resource-gated super attacks with anime-inspired visuals
- Memory Shards: screenshot → pin to map as visual reminder; addresses cognitive load of tracking locked paths; inspired by player photo-mode behavior; required cross-platform prototyping
- Guided Mode: optional objective markers + obstacle warnings; designed so disabled players can opt out of navigation challenge while keeping boss/combat difficulty
- Accessibility integrated from project inception (~2–3 years dedicated); senior designer Remi Boutin: "accessible design is good design"
- Metacritic: ~85; won Pégases 2025 (France) Game of the Year
- Critical consensus: one of the best Metroidvanias in recent years

## Cross-cutting observations

1. **Tool-as-traversal-key + combat-tool** convergence: Silksong (tools), Iconoclasts (wrench), Lost Crown (Time Powers) all collapse "traversal ability" and "combat move" into the same pickup. Shantae and Aeterna Noctis keep them partially separate.
2. **Map annotation** has emerged as a genre friction point; Lost Crown's Memory Shards is the current state-of-art solution.
3. **Difficulty layering** (Aeterna Noctis's Noctis Mode, Lost Crown's Guided Mode) shows two opposite directions: one adds precision challenge on top; the other removes navigation friction on top. Both preserve their base game's identity.
4. **Solo-dev durability** (Iconoclasts, ~7–10 years, Construct Classic) shows raw persistence beats engine sophistication for shipping — but also that scope control is the real risk.
