### Last Epoch
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Last_Epoch — Unity engine, Kickstarter 2018 ($250K+), EA April 2019, full release Feb 21 2024; 5 classes (Acolyte/Mage/Primalist/Rogue/Sentinel) × 3 masteries each = 15 archetypes; sold 2.135M by late March 2024; 264,708 peak concurrent Steam users; macOS/Linux cancelled; multiplayer beta March 2023
- https://www.pcgamesn.com/last-epoch/review — 8/10 review; mastery system (permanent choice, each has signature skill); crafting "vastly more directed and manageable" than PoE; Monolith of Fate (web of Echoes), Arena, 3 dungeons (Lightless Arbor, Soulfire Bastion, Temporal Sanctum); Cycle seasonal system; co-op 4 players; "middle ground between D4 and PoE2"
