## Dreams PS4

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dreams_(video_game)
- PS4 exclusive, released February 14, 2020. Developer: Media Molecule. Publisher: Sony Interactive Entertainment.
- Creation via the "Imp" cursor (DualShock 4 gyro / Move controllers / analog sticks).
- DreamShaping suite: Assembly Mode (stamp elements), Gadgets (node-graph logic), Edit Mode (sculpt, sound, lighting, animation), cooperative creation.
- DreamSurfing: curated playlists ("Mm Picks," "New Trending Dreams"), thumbs-up ratings, Community Jam contests.
- Remixing: any creation can be extracted and modified by any player.
- Live service support ended September 2023; servers remained online. Curation ended April 2024.
- Metacritic: 89/100.

**Source:** https://www.inverse.com/gaming/dreams-anniversary-five-years-playstation-media-molecule
- Commercially underperformed despite critical acclaim. "Marketed to the absolutely wrong audience."
- Identity crisis from 2015 reveal: unclear whether product was a game or a toolchain.
- Campaign didn't effectively demonstrate the creation tools' scope.
- No PC port ever materialised; PS4 lock-in limited revival potential.
- No public sales figures ever disclosed by PlayStation.

**Source:** https://www.gamedeveloper.com/marketing/ad-infinitum-handling-community-curation-on-a-cosmic-scale-in-dreams
- Editorial manager Jen Simpkins: hybrid manual + algorithmic curation.
- Manual: team trawls "New" feed; monitors #DreamsPS4, #MadeInDreams, Twitch, Reddit.
- Algorithmic: in-game sort by likes/trending; play-history-based playlist recommendations introduced 2024.
- Five editorial criteria: Realism, Polish, Novelty, Functionality, Heart.
- Recognition: Mm Picks badges, Impy Awards, The Impsider blog, weekly streams.
- Key lesson: diverse curation sources prevent echo chambers; intentionally surface beginners.

**Source:** https://docs.indreams.me/en/whats-happening/news/curation2024
- Curation team made redundant as part of studio restructuring (live service ended Sep 2023).
- DreamSurfing now uses algorithmic recommendations based on recent play history.
- Genre shelves, Featured Playlists Archive, and Community Curators playlist remain.

**Source:** https://www.psfanatic.com/ps4s-dreams-to-lose-curation-support-but-no-plans-for-it-to-go-offline/
- Media Molecule confirmed: no plans to take servers offline.
- Live curation ended April 2024.

## Core (Manticore Games)

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Core_(video_game)
- Developer: Manticore Games. Platform: Windows (free-to-play). Built on Unreal Engine 4.
- Closed alpha: December 2019. Public open alpha: March 2020. Early Access: April 2021.
- Creation: drag-and-drop kitbashing, asset library, .obj import, Lua scripting against built-in API.
- Supports up to 32-player multiplayer. Games cannot be exported as standalone; hosted within Core client.
- December 2020: Mining Magnate and Roll 'Em first titles to reach 100,000 plays.
- Revenue split: 50% to creators (subscription, IAP, pay-to-play levers).
- September 2025: Manticore shipped "Out of Time" (co-op roguelite) to Epic Games Store, built on Core infrastructure.

**Source:** https://www.gamesround.com/blog/manticore-games-raises-100m-for-ugc-games-platform-core
- Raised $100 M Series C led by XN; SoftBank and London Venture Partners participated.
- CEO Frederic Descamps: "Core puts the power in creators' hands to build an endless arcade of free games for players."
- Manticore does not develop games itself; provides infrastructure, economic system, and collaboration tools.

**Source:** https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2025-deep-dive/
- Core's most-played title (Island Survival Alpha, 2M plays) last updated 2023.
- Leadership recruiting for a proprietary MMO — platform shifting toward first-party content.
- Broader UGC market: Roblox 82.93M DAU (+21% YoY); Fortnite Creative 70k creators (tripled from 2023).
- Creator payouts across major platforms grew 32% YoY.

**Source:** https://techcrunch.com/2021/10/14/core-manticore-metaverse-game-creation-hands-on/
- "Core might be the Vegas of the metaverse."
- Unreal-quality graphics differentiated from Roblox's aesthetic.
- Editor resembles lightweight Unreal: move/rotate/scale gizmos, content browser, play-in-editor.

## LittleBigPlanet 3

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LittleBigPlanet_3
- Developer: Sumo Digital (with Media Molecule, Sony XDev). Platforms: PS3, PS4. Released November 2014.
- 16 depth layers (up from 3 in LBP1/2).
- Dynamic Thermometer: streams objects in/out based on proximity, enabling denser scenes.
- 70 total tools. Adventures format: multi-level experiences with shared progression.
- Four characters: Sackboy (original), OddSock (wall-jump), Swoop (flight), Toggle (size-shift).
- Up to 4-player co-op.
- Metacritic: 79/100. Praised for level design and character variety; criticised for technical issues and brief campaign.
- PS4 servers shut down permanently April 19, 2024. Over 10 million user-created levels lost.

**Source:** https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/sony-confirms-littlebigplanet-3-shutdown-months-after-it-was-temporarily-taken-offline/
- Servers taken "temporarily" offline January 8, 2024 due to technical issues.
- Permanent shutdown confirmed April 19, 2024 — no prior public warning to allow community archival.
- Locally saved levels playable offline; server-stored content permanently lost.

**Source:** https://kotaku.com/littlebigplanet-3-ps4-servers-sony-playstation-1851423658
- Community preservation groups attempted emergency downloads but time window was too short.
- "Death of creativity" framing in press; widely cited as a game preservation failure case.

**Source:** https://delistedgames.com/littlebigplanet-3/
- Game delisted from PlayStation Store October 31, 2024.
- No sequel announced; series considered effectively ended.

**Source:** https://littlebigplanet.fandom.com/wiki/Create_Mode
- Play + Create + Share loop: hold Square anywhere in Community Moon to enter Create Mode.
- Level creation tools include material brushes, stickers, sound objects, circuit boards (logic).
- Community Moon: searchable level hub with rating/play counts and Mm Heart endorsements.

## Planet Coaster 2

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planet_Coaster_2
- Developer/Publisher: Frontier Developments. Platforms: Windows (Steam), PS5, Xbox Series X/S.
- Released November 6, 2024. Metacritic: 76/100.
- Pathing: piece-by-piece replaced by draw system.
- Event Sequencer: groups objects into timeline, triggers animations and effects.
- Blueprints: save/load arbitrary object groups.
- Object scaling, mirroring/twinning, scenery attachment to rides.
- Cross-platform co-op; shared saves for co-building (Franchise Mode).
- Career, Sandbox, and Franchise (online competitive/co-op) modes.
- Management: guest satisfaction, staff, finances, heat-map analytics.

**Source:** https://www.planetcoaster.com/en-US/player-guides/coaster-ride-building
- Excitement driven by G-forces, inversions, speed variation, "head chopper" near-miss moments.
- Terrain tools: sculpt/flatten/chisel/paint brushes, variable size and intensity.

**Source:** https://www.planetcoaster.com/en-US/player-guides/blueprints
- Blueprints usable in-game and via Frontier Workshop.
- Sharecode system for cross-platform blueprint lookup.

**Source:** https://www.planetcoaster.com/workshop
- Frontier Workshop: 70,000+ community blueprints, browsable by theme and coaster type.

**Source:** https://www.planetcoaster.com/news/2025-03-06/spring-2025-roadmap-update-frontier-unlocked-overview
- Update 3 (March 2025): Featured Creators list, follower alerts for new uploads, What's Hot leaderboard.
- First algorithmic discovery layer added post-launch.

**Source:** https://www.pcgamesn.com/planet-coaster-2/strategy-game-design
- "Feels like the ultimate blend of strategy game design" — creation praised; management criticised as shallow.
- Management mechanics lack depth relative to building tools' sophistication.

## Rec Room

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rec_Room_(video_game)
- Launch: June 1, 2016. Platforms: PS (PSVR), Xbox, Nintendo Switch, Meta Quest, iOS, Android, PC Steam.
- Maker Pen: 3D draw tool with ink budget (per-room computation limit); clone, recolour, constrain objects.
- Circuits V1: simple event wiring. Circuits V2: full visual programming language, typed ports.
- Rec Room Studio (2022): Unity-based desktop authoring; asset import, custom animations.
- Free-to-play; sustained by RR+ subscription and cosmetic sales.
- Creator Tokens: earned via Rec Room Originals or RR+ subscriptions; exchangeable for real money.
- AIRS: automated moderation system; 96%+ compliance rate on tens of thousands of daily inventions.

**Source:** https://roadtovr.com/rec-room-layoff-half-staff-august-2025/
- August 2025: ~50% workforce laid off (staff reduced to ~100). Earlier March 2025: 16% cut.
- Reason: overambitious cross-platform scale; mobile/console creators produced volume, not quality.
- Co-founder Cameron Brown: "we got crushed under the scale."
- Strategic pivot: narrow to elite PC creators using Studio + mouse+keyboard.
- Mobile repositioned as consumption, not creation tier.
- July 2025: record user spending on UGC; creators' highest-earning month, crossing $1M quarterly.

**Source:** https://blog.recroom.com/posts/maker-ai
- Maker AI: voice-activated AI assistant; spatially aware of room; access to millions of library items.
- Can spawn/place/alter objects, set lighting, create object behaviours, define game rules.
- Works on any platform with a microphone.
- Initially in personal dorm rooms only; unrestricted access requires RR+ (computational cost).
- No public user testing data; aspirational framing ("fascinatingly powerful and also frustratingly unpredictable").

**Source:** https://sacra.com/research/rec-room/
- Creator-made avatar items outsell Rec Room's first-party avatar content by Q3 2025.
- Revenue split: ~70 cents per $1 from first-party content; ~30 cents per $1 from UGC.
- Makers AI launched March 2025; early test users averaged 35+ minutes/session and 300+ prompts.

**Source:** https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2025-deep-dive/
- Rec Room absent from 2025 UGC platform rankings (dominated by Roblox/Fortnite).
- Broader market: AI-powered creation tools cited as next frontier for democratising game dev.
