## Tibia
Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibia_(video_game) — launch 1997, CipSoft, 2D tile oblique engine, vocations, death penalty, skull PvP system, 68 servers, peak 64k concurrent, Brazil/Poland demographics

- https://www.mmobomb.com/why-do-so-many-mmorpg-fans-long-tibia — design philosophy: no quest markers, no hand-holding, community knowledge-sharing as social glue
- https://mmohuts.com/review/tibia — PvP server types, skull system, death penalty as design feature

Key facts captured:
- Launched Jan 7, 1997; one of the earliest graphical MMORPGs
- Rune magic creates cross-class economic dependency (Sorcerers/Druids craft consumables others buy)
- No level cap (unlimited progression); Level 999 door sealed for 19 years
- Custom 2D engine; technically lightweight enabling small-team maintenance for 29 years
- Peak 64,028 concurrent (Nov 2007); large Brazilian and Polish player bases

- Player vending shops (Merchant class) fill towns; no mandatory NPC auction house
- War of Emperium twice weekly; castle ownership grants treasury access
- Rebirth at 99/50 → Transcendent jobs → Third Classes; genuine power reward not just cosmetic reset
- Anime adaptation 2004; mobile game 2018; IP cultural durability demonstrated

Key facts captured:
- Class switching in lobby; each class leveled independently; subclass adds 20% stats + abilities
- Mag system: hovering droid raised by feeding items; triggers support abilities; persists across class switches
- Urgent Quests: timed announcements draw community to same content simultaneously
- Per-character private loot in co-op instances (no loot drama)
- Cosmetic gacha (Scratch Tickets) + player-to-player resale market in Meseta (in-game currency)
- Global launch May 2020; Japanese-only for 8 years
- New Genesis (2021) replaced instanced dungeons with open-world combat

- Global adaptation: autoplay removed; 3 million players week 1, 300k concurrent peak; server consolidation 107→25 by Feb 2025

## Wakfu
Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakfu — 2012, Ankama, Java engine, turn-based tactical, 18 classes, Krosmoz universe
- https://krosmoz.fandom.com/wiki/Wakfu_(MMO) — class and spell details; Riktus Clan added 2014
- https://www.wakfu.com/en/mmorpg/discover — official overview: ecology + politics as headline features
- https://www.wakfu.com/en/mmorpg/tutorials/425008-ecosystem — ecology mechanics: population tracking, seed planting, species extinction, Ecologist role
- https://www.wakfu.com/en/mmorpg/tutorials/425665-politics — governance: Citizenship Points, Governor elections every 2 weeks, ministerial appointments, tax setting, species protection laws
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/215080/WAKFU/ — Steam listing; free-to-play with subscription tier

Key facts captured:
- Turn-based grid combat: Action Points for movement, Points for actions, 30-second turn limit
- 18 classes; all spells granted early; progression = mastery of existing kit not unlock of new spells
- Ecology: monster/plant populations tracked per zone; over-harvest causes permanent depletion; Trapper/Lumberjack professions reintroduce species
- Political governance: 4 Nations, 2-week Governor elections, ministers (Ecologist/Treasurer/military), tax rates, national budgets
- Riktus Clan (criminal nation) gains bonuses by destroying ecology — structured ideological opposition
- Wakfu animated series (2008+) predates and promotes the MMO; shared Krosmoz universe

## Spiral Knights
Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiral_Knights — 2011, Three Rings Design/Sega, Java, won Best Online Game Design GDC Online 2011
- https://wiki.spiralknights.com/Clockworks — tier structure: T1/T2/T3 separated by Moorcroft Manor and Emberlight; depth 1–28; daily reconfiguration
- https://wiki.spiralknights.com/Heat — heat level 1–10; fire crystals per tier; heat meter fills through combat use
- https://mmokb.com/spiral-knights-depth-farming-guide/ — T3 endgame: best materials, best Crown-per-run ratio; equipment star-rating gate per tier
- https://mmokb.com/spiral-knights-crafting-alchemy-guide/ — alchemy recipe system; materials + Orbs of Alchemy + Crowns; must reach heat 10 before star upgrade
- https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/SpiralKnights — visual identity (mecha-medieval hybrid), co-op design, no-roles party structure

Key facts captured:
- Three weapon categories (sword/gun/bomb); four damage types (normal/elemental/piercing/shadow); armor star ratings 0–5
- No experience levels; all progression through gear star rating
- Heat system: use gear in combat → fill heat meter → at level 10 → alchemize to next star with materials + Orbs
- Clockworks: procedurally assembled from room pool, daily rotation; depth determines tier and loot quality
- Haven hub: persistent social space; Arcade = dungeon gates; Garrison = guild halls, Lab, Hall of Heroes
- 1–4 player squads; no role requirements; frictionless co-op entry
- Energy system criticism (blocked floor transitions) → redesigned July 2013
- 1 million accounts in 3 months; 5+ million by 2018; studio → Grey Havens 2016; Java updated to 21 by 2026
