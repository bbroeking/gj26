### Overwatch (2016)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overwatch_(2016_video_game)
  - 2016 release; 21 heroes at launch, 32 by April 2020; 7M players week 1, 10M by June 2016; loot box cosmetics; Overwatch League city-franchise model announced BlizzCon 2016.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Development_of_Overwatch
  - Team 4 at Blizzard; evolved out of cancelled MMO Titan; hero designs derived from Titan class archetypes.
- https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1024001/-Overwatch-Gameplay-Architecture-and
  - GDC 2017: Prometheus ECS architecture; 63 Hz server tick; client-authoritative netcode with shooter-first priority; interpolation/extrapolation.
- https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-influences-and-differences-of-team-fortress-2-classes-and-overwatch-heroes
  - TF2 nine-class origin; OW extends to active abilities + ultimates; MOBA-influenced design.

### Apex Legends (2019)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apex_Legends
  - Released Feb 4 2019; Respawn/EA; Source engine (modified); 25 legends / 5 classes; 25M players week 1; 100M by April 2021; $2B revenue by May 2022; 20 squads of 3.
- https://departmentofplay.net/deconstructing-respawns-battle-royale-apex-legends/
  - Design analysis: borrows Fortnite aesthetic + PUBG realism; automated loot equipping; 60-player matches; no fall damage; respawn beacon mechanic detailed.
- https://www.pcgamer.com/apex-legends-ping-system-is-a-tiny-miracle-for-fps-teamwork-and-communication/
  - "Gold standard for non-verbal communication"; context-sensitive voice lines; controller single-button; removes voice-chat dependency.
- https://uxdesign.cc/apex-legends-ping-system-gaming-ux-done-right-4661cd94954c
  - UX analysis: accessibility, language barriers, toxicity reduction.

### Fortnite (2017)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortnite_Battle_Royale
  - Released Sep 26 2017; Epic Games; Unreal Engine 4 → UE5 (Dec 2021); 100-player battle royale; battle pass from Season 2 (Dec 2017); 350M registered players by May 2020; Marshmello concert 10M concurrent; V-Bucks currency.
- https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/q-a-how-epic-pared-down-i-fortnite-battle-royale-i-to-be-fast-and-approachable

- https://dev.epicgames.com/docs/epic-account-services/crossplay/crossplay-technical-overview
  - EOS crossplay SDK; unified session pools across PC/console/mobile.

### Destiny 2 (2017)
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Destiny_2
  - Released Sep 6 2017 (console), Oct 24 2017 (PC); Bungie; PvE + PvP; 6-player raids; 10 expansions 2017–2024; F2P since 2019; The Final Shape June 2024 concluded saga; live service ended June 2026.
- https://www.pcgamer.com/the-strange-science-of-destiny-2s-uniquely-complicated-netcode/
  - Hybrid P2P + client-server; Activity Host at 10 Hz; combat damage at 30 Hz; DDOS exposure; host-migration issues.
- https://bungie.fandom.com/wiki/Tiger_Engine
  - Tiger engine derived from Halo's Blam!; proprietary Bungie tech; powers open patrol zones and cinematics.
- https://naavik.co/digest/bungie-destiny-2-live-service-model/
  - Content sunsetting backlash; expansion model costs; Sony $766M write-down; layoffs.
- https://solismagazine.com/destiny-2s-end-on-june-9-and-the-9-year-saga-that-defined-live-service-shooters
  - Nine-year retrospective; progenitor of live-service FPS-MMO hybrid; community-built endgame (LFG, lore channels, fashion shows in Tower).
