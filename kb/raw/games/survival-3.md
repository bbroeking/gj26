## Green Hell

- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Hell_(video_game)
  - Developer: Creepy Jar (Warsaw-based indie); Engine: Unity
  - Release: Windows Sep 5 2019; Switch Oct 2020; PS4/XB1 Jun 2021; PS5/XBSeries Aug 2024
  - Sales: 1M by Jun 2020; 6M by Jun 2024. Metacritic 78–84/100.
  - Up to 4 players co-op (no PvP). Story Mode co-op added v1.6.0.
  - Sanity system: -35 sanity per co-op player death.
  - Mechanics: multi-axis survival (nutrition macros via smartwatch, psyche/sanity, body map for injuries, sleep). Wound care includes maggot therapy for infections.
  - Crafting: drag-and-drop in backpack; notebook fills in discovered recipes.
  - Setting: Amazon rainforest; story follows Jake searching for missing wife Mia.
  - Sources: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3256423835; https://nerdschalk.com/is-green-hell-multiplayer/

## Raft

- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raft_(video_game)
  - Developer: Redbeet Interactive; Publisher: Axolot Games; Engine: Unity
  - Early access: May 23 2018 (free browser version existed before); full release: Jun 20 2022
  - Console: PS5/XBSeries Dec 4 2024. Up to 10 players co-op, cross-platform voice chat.
  - Sales: 5M+ by 2024; #1 Steam bestseller two consecutive weeks at 1.0 launch.
  - Core mechanic: hook tool pulls debris from ocean current. Everything built from salvage before it drifts past.
  - Progression: research table unlocks recipes as new materials are encountered.
  - World: procedurally generated infinite ocean; scattered islands with lore/puzzles; radio receiver guides narrative.
  - Story: post-apocalyptic climate-changed Earth; narrative islands with character backstory.
  - Threats: shark attacks raft planks if not distracted; thirst/hunger; storms.
  - Sources: https://www.co-op.gg/pc/game/raft; https://store.steampowered.com/app/648800/Raft/

## 7 Days to Die

- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_Days_to_Die
  - Developer: The Fun Pimps; Engine: Unity
  - Early access: Dec 13 2013 (one of longest EA runs); full 1.0 release: Jul 25 2024
  - Platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, PS4/5, XB1/Series. Console Metacritic: 35–45/100 (PC much better).
  - Co-op: player-hosted servers and LAN; no official dedicated server cap cited but community typically up to 8.
  - Horde Night: Blood Moon every 7th in-game night — massive zombie horde attacks your position; escalates in difficulty with each cycle.
  - World gen: random seed worlds across biomes OR fixed Navezgane, Arizona map.
  - Building: voxel-based fully destructible; structural stability — buildings collapse if load-bearing blocks destroyed.
  - Skill system: five attributes (Perception, Strength, Fortitude, Agility, Intellect) → skill trees underneath.
  - Crafting: broad menu system; tiered materials gating progression.
  - Zombies: speed/strength scale with game-day count, not just horde night.
  - Sources: https://7daystodie.com/; https://www.corrosionhour.com/7-days-to-die-review-survival-horde-crafter/

## DayZ

- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DayZ_(video_game)
  - Developer: Bohemia Interactive; Engine: started on Real Virtuality branch of Arma 2, gradually migrating to in-house Enfusion engine post-release
  - Standalone release: Dec 13 2018 (Windows); console 2019
  - Origins: Arma 2 mod by Dean Hall (2012), became its own product
  - Sales: 4M+ during early access; Sep 2024 peak 78,739 concurrent Steam players
  - Server cap: 64 simultaneous players on a 200–300 km² map (Chernarus, based on Czech Republic geography)
  - Core design: permadeath — lose all gear on death; no respawn preserving progress
  - Loot: scarcity-driven; spawn with almost nothing; military gear in dedicated zones
  - Persistence: items, shelters, vehicles persist on server even when player offline (server-side)
  - Player interactions: proximity voice chat (silence = suspicious); no NPC quest givers; all drama emergent
  - Dynamic events: helicopter crashes, train wrecks, military convoys spawn rare loot
  - Health: bleeding, fractures, infections, temperature — complex multi-axis injury system
  - Genre influence: direct predecessor of Rust, SCUM, Escape from Tarkov
  - Sources: https://doctorspin.net/dayz-for-days/; https://ggservers.com/blog/how-dayz-revolutionised-the-gaming-world-with-real-facts/; https://www.researchgate.net/publication/320646682_Fear_loss_and_meaningful_play_Permadeath_in_DayZ

## Astroneer

- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astroneer
  - Developer: System Era Softworks; Engine: Unreal Engine 4
  - Early access: Dec 16 2016; full release: Feb 6 2019 (Windows, XB1); PS4; Switch; PS5 Nov 2025
  - Sales: 3.74M units by Mar 2022; 8M+ players
  - Co-op: 4 players on listen server; up to 8 on dedicated headless server; cross-play
  - Core innovation: Terrain Tool — real-time voxel-like terrain deformation for both resource gathering and shaping. Built from day one as the central mechanic.
  - Backpack: IS the HUD. 2 quick slots, 8 storage slots, integrated small printer, oxygen bar. No traditional inventory screen.
  - Crafting: spatial hologram UI (blue icons) — no text menus; shows required materials as objects
  - Power: gates everything (terrain tool, printers, base modules). Must craft tethers/batteries/generators.
  - Progression: 7 planets (Sylva → Desolo → Calidor → Vesania → Novus → Glacio → Atrox); each has unique resources and hazards. Gateway Chambers on each planet feed into a central Gateway Engine activation.
  - No combat: hazards are environmental (atmosphere, lack of power, falling)
  - Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-an-engaging-and-intuitive-crafting-system-for-i-astroneer-i-; https://astroneer.fandom.com/wiki/Multiplayer

## Enshrouded

- Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshrouded
  - Developer: Keen Games; Engine: not publicly specified (proprietary)
  - Early access: Jan 24 2024; full release scheduled 2026
  - Sales: 1M players in first week; 500K copies first 3 days; 5M players by Jan 2026
  - Co-op: up to 16 players simultaneously (dedicated server)
  - Core mechanic: Shroud — magical fog covering parts of the world. Lethal if players stay inside too long (5 min early game). Upgrading Flame Altar extends timer. Best loot is inside the Shroud.
  - Flame Altar: crafted from 5 stones; placed anywhere; serves as respawn anchor and progression milestone tracker
  - NPC system: 5 core Craftspeople (Blacksmith Oswald Anders, Alchemist Balthazar, Farmer, Hunter, Carpenter). Rescued from Ancient Vaults (small rescue dungeons) using Summoning Staff, then installed at base. Each unlocks a class of crafting station and recipes. Quests guide player to each in sequence.
  - Building: voxel-based; terrain manipulation; multi-story; 16-player co-op can build simultaneously
  - Skill tree: three archetypes (Melee, Ranged, Magic); hybrids viable; respec available at cost
  - Well Rested buff: roofed enclosed shelter grants stamina regen + XP bonus (parallels Valheim's Rested)
  - Sources: https://deltiasgaming.com/enshrouded-how-to-unlock-all-craftspeople-and-survivors/; https://minmax.blog/articles/published/enshrouded-release/; https://gamerant.com/enshrouded-best-craftspeople-locations/; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshrouded
