## Garry's Mod

### Source: Wikipedia — Garry's Mod
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garry%27s_Mod

- Created by Garry Newman; originated as a Source mod (December 2004); standalone release November 2006
- Platforms: Windows, Mac OS X (2010), Linux (2013)
- Core tools: Physics Gun (pick up/rotate objects) + Tool Gun (weld, constrain, script)
- Scripting: Lua; full entity, physics, UI, and network access
- Steam Workshop replaced Toybox system; commercial scripts now on GmodStore (formerly ScriptFodder)
- Notable community modes: Trouble in Terrorist Town (winner 2009 Fretta Contest), DarkRP, Prop Hunt
- Prop Hunt mechanic later appeared in Call of Duty, Fortnite, Genshin Impact
- Sales: 25.5 million copies by November 2024; revenue ~$119.8M by December 2020
- Guinness World Records: best-selling PC-exclusive game (September 2024)
- Successor: S&box (Facepunch, released April 2026) — Source 2 / UE4

### Source: gamephilosophy.org — A Game Made From Other Games (PCG 2017)
URL: https://www.gamephilosophy.org/wp-content/uploads/confmanuscripts/pcg2017/Nelson%20-%202017%20-%20A%20Game%20Made%20From%20Other%20Games%20Actions%20and%20Entities%20in%20Garrys%20Mod.pdf

- Academic paper analyzing GMod as a "game made from other games" using Source assets
- Argues that GMod's content is entirely parasitic on Source ecosystem games the player already owns

## Second Life

### Source: Wikipedia — Second Life
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Life

- Launched publicly June 23, 2003; developer: Linden Lab (Philip Rosedale, founded 1999)
- Users retain copyright for content they create (explicit policy from launch)
- TOS modification August 2013: Linden Lab granted itself a "non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free" license for promotional use — controversy
- Linden Dollar (L$): closed-loop virtual currency; officially "has no monetary value" outside platform; exchangeable via LindeX → PayPal
- Economy: $3.6M monthly (Sept 2005) → est. $500M GDP annually by 2015; over $3.2B transactions by 2013 (avg 1.2M daily)
- Peak concurrent: 88,200 avatars (early 2009)
- Server: each region on a dedicated core on Debian Linux; Havok physics
- Scripting: Linden Scripting Language (LSL) — event-driven, C-like

### Source: gamesbeat.com — Linden Lab has spent $1.3B building Second Life and paid $1.1B to creators
URL: https://gamesbeat.com/linden-lab-has-spent-1-3b-building-second-life-and-paid-1-1b-to-creators/

- Linden Lab invested $1.3B building SL since 2003; paid out $1.1B to creators
- Revenue share: 90% to creators, 10% to Linden Lab
- ~160 employees running the platform on that margin
- Active users ~600,000; avg retention ~14 years on platform

### Source: modemworld.me — Second Life: $1.3B to build; $1.1B paid to creators (Dec 2024)
URL: https://modemworld.me/2024/12/20/second-life-1-3b-to-build-1-1b-paid-to-creators/

- 2023 creator income: 21,152 creators earned; 30% earned >$1K; 7.4% earned >$10K; 139 earned >$100K; 14 became millionaires
- "Integrity Fees" and increased commissions for high-volume merchants debated 2025–2026

## Habbo Hotel

### Source: digiday.com — Before Meta, there was Habbo
URL: https://digiday.com/marketing/before-meta-there-was-habbo-how-social-games-laid-the-framework-for-the-metaverse/

- Launched 2000; developer: Sulake (Finland)
- Isometric pixel-art social hotel; public rooms + player-owned private rooms
- Furni (furniture) economy: buy credits with real money → buy Furni → trade player-to-player
- Secondary market emerged organically around rare/discontinued Furni
- 2008 peak: $74M revenue; 16.5M monthly unique users
- Habbo 2020 Unity reboot: omitting item trading caused user revolt → trading restored
- 2012 Channel 4 exposé: predatory behavior; post-2012: 24/7 monitoring, word filters, mute tools
- VICE 2012 article documented exploitation issues

### Source: gurlworld.com — What Happened to Habbo? Habbo Hotel Turns 25
URL: https://gurlworld.com/what-happened-to-habbo-habbo-hotel-turns-25/

- 320 million total accounts created since 2000
- 850,000 monthly active users (2024)
- Core demographic now 20+ years old; original users grew up with platform

### Source: jgonzalezferrer.substack.com — What made Habbo successful
URL: https://jgonzalezferrer.substack.com/p/what-made-habbo-successful-virtual

- Identifies Furni trading, room identity, and public social spaces as three pillars of retention
- Notes that the simplicity of the isometric tile system lowered creation friction vs. full 3D

## VRChat

### Source: Wikipedia — VRChat
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VRChat

- Created by Graham Gaylor and Jesse Joudrey; launched January 16, 2014 for Oculus Rift DK1
- Steam Early Access: February 2017; Meta Quest: December 2018; iOS/Android: October 24, 2025
- Engine: Unity; avatars/worlds uploaded via VRChat SDK
- Avatar features: head/hand IK, lip-syncing, eye tracking, blinking, finger tracking
- Creator Economy (May 2023): group subscriptions using VRChat Credits; groups receive 50% of revenue
- Avatar Marketplace (May 2025): licensed avatars (e.g., Quill from Moss)
- Trust Rank system: earned via time, friends, uploads; gates permissions (show custom avatars)
- Avatar file size limit (July 2024): 200 MB compressed / 500 MB VRAM decompressed
- Concurrent users: ~120,000 weekend peak 2025; New Year's Eve 2025–26: ~149,000
- Record: ~156,700 concurrent during Sanrio concert, February 15, 2026
- Communities: 58,000+ Trans Academy members; Deaf community using sign language; "Mutes" culture

### Source: creators.vrchat.com — Getting Started / Roadmap
URL: https://creators.vrchat.com/getting-started/

- SDK-based world/avatar creation; requires Unity knowledge
- "Only a small subset of skilled users create avatars and worlds" — acknowledged bottleneck
- Roadmap page indicates ongoing Creator Economy expansion

### Source: hello.vrchat.com
URL: https://hello.vrchat.com/
