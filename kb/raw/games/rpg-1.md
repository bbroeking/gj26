## Game 1: The Elder Scrolls V Skyrim (2011, Bethesda)

### Source 1: Designophiles — Design Analysis: Skyrim
URL: https://designophiles.wordpress.com/2017/02/10/design-analysis-the-elder-scrolls-v-skyrim/

- 18 skill trees, use-based leveling (skill XP gained by using the skill, not killing enemies)
- Each character level-up grants 1 perk point + choice to raise Magicka/Health/Stamina by 10
- Race selection only at character creation — no class, no primary skills
- World reactivity limitation: guard dialogue acknowledges guild but NPCs ignore multi-faction leadership
- Design lesson: "simplicity in core mechanics enables complexity elsewhere"
- Re-release: Special Edition (October 2016, PS4/Xbox One/PC) with 64-bit engine, improved lighting, console mod support

### Source 2: Terminally Incoherent — Skyrim Radiant Quest System
URL: https://www.terminally-incoherent.com/blog/2011/12/16/skyrim-radiant-quest-system/

- Radiant system generates quests by reading player state (level, visited locations, guild membership) and slotting NPC names/dungeon targets procedurally
- "Nearly all radiant quests were fully voiced" — key to making procedural content feel authored
- Dark Brotherhood assassination contracts use a "large sound bank of recorded lines and clever sound editing"
- Criticism: radiant quests create permanent quest markers that can't be removed, frustrating completionists
- Some commenters note Skyrim simplified significantly from Morrowind's stat complexity

### Source 3: Elder Scrolls Fandom Wiki — The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim
URL: https://elderscrolls.fandom.com/wiki/The_Elder_Scrolls_V:_Skyrim

- Released worldwide November 11, 2011 (PC/PS3/Xbox 360)
- Action RPG, first-person and third-person
- 18 interconnected skill trees organized under three guardian signs (constellation visual metaphor)
- ~180 perks (251 counting multi-rank perks)
- Thu'um shout system: absorb dragon souls → unlock shouts in the dragon language

### Source 4: Gamereactor — Skyrim 30 million copies
URL: https://www.gamereactor.eu/skyrim-has-sold-30-million-copies-worldwide/

- 30 million copies across all platforms by 2016

### Source 5: GamesRadar — Skyrim $1 billion launch revenue
URL: https://www.gamesradar.com/skyrim-might-have-actually-made-dollar1-billion-at-launch-thats-dollar350m-more-than-was-previously-thought/

- Generated ~$1 billion in revenue in first 30 days (previously reported as $650M; revised upward)
- Shipped 7 million copies in week one

### Search — Skyrim Creation Engine
- Built on "Creation Engine," a rebuild of Gamebryo
- Perk tree UI uses constellation star-map visual; 18 skills mapped to three guardian sign groups

## Game 2: The Witcher 3 Wild Hunt (2015, CD Projekt Red)

### Source 1: Destructoid — CD Projekt Red designer breaks down best quest
URL: https://www.destructoid.com/cd-projekt-red-designer-breaks-down-the-witcher-3s-best-quest/

- Pawel Sasko (Lead Technical Quest Designer) presented at Digital Dragons conference; also GDC 2017
- Color-coded pacing graph: blue = non-interactive cutscene, red = combat, green = interactive dialogue/walking
- "Most important parts in the story are when the tension is the lowest and when the tension is the highest"
- "When you have too much of the gameplay, you start to lose purpose"
- Botchling burial in Bloody Baron is the emotional catharsis peak; players frequently cry
- Design principle: avoid clichés by crossing them off the brainstorm list during iteration
- Sasko on the Baron: "There are very few people in our lives, even if we love them, that we've never felt conflicted about"

### Source 2: The Ringer — Witcher 3 10-year anniversary
URL: https://www.theringer.com/2025/05/16/video-games/the-witcher-3-10-year-anniversary-open-world-storytelling-quests-bloody-baron

- Bloody Baron questline officially called "Family Matters"
- Branching starts at how player approaches Crow's Perch; choices influence long-term Velen consequences
- Some of best writing in side quests and contracts, not just the main quest

### Source 3: Fextralife Witcher 3 Wiki — Builds
URL: https://thewitcher3.wiki.fextralife.com/Builds

- Five Signs: Aard (telekinetic blast/knockback), Igni (fire stream), Quen (shield, absorbs one hit, alt heals), Yrden (magic trap/slow), Axii (mind control)
- Three skill trees: Combat (fast/heavy attacks, adrenaline), Signs, Alchemy
- Mutagen/ability slot system: green mutagens boost vitality; red boost attack; slot adjacent to matching skill for 50% bonus
- Example archetypes: Sign build (permanent Quen), Alchemy tank (Heightened Tolerance → 3-4 decoctions), Cat School (dodge/glass cannon), Bear School (tanky/alchemy)

### Source 4: Windows Central / GameRant — 50 million sales
URL: https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-crosses-50-million-copies-sold

- Over 50 million copies sold by 2023

### Source 5: 80.lv — REDengine 3 interview
URL: https://80.lv/articles/the-witcher-3-interview-a-deeper-look-into-redengine-3

- REDengine 3 built specifically for large-scale open world environments across multiple platforms
- Development: 3.5 years, $81 million budget, self-funded, 250 in-house devs + ~1,500 external

### Search — Gwent
- Gwent became a standalone free-to-play card game (Gwent: The Witcher Card Game, 2018)
- Embedded in-world as something citizens naturally play; collecting cards is a questline

## Game 3: Baldur's Gate 3 (2023, Larian Studios)

### Source 1: RPG Gazette — Rolling Virtual Dice: Transmedial Adaptation of D&D 5e in BG3
URL: https://therpggazette.wordpress.com/2025/06/27/rolling-virtual-dice-transmedial-adaptation-of-dd-5e-in-baldurs-gate-3/

- Adapts D&D 5e for video game — not a literal port; adapts for solo/co-op digital experience
- Visually represents ability score modifiers, proficiency bonuses, and d20 rolls
- 9 races, 13 classes, flexible ability score placement (optional 5e rule implemented as default)
- DM function is automated: perception checks, DC adjudication handled by system, not a human
- Narrators fills DM's descriptive role (voiced)
- Modding enables custom spells, classes — mirrors tabletop homebrewing
- Optional 5e rules (flexible ability scores) made defaults; multiclassing proficiency gains restricted for clarity

### Source 2: GameRant — BG3 vs D&D 5e similarities/differences
URL: https://gamerant.com/baldurs-gate-3-dungeons-dragons-5e-different-same/

- Turn-based combat matches 5e: team initiative, advantage/disadvantage, attacks of opportunity
- Narrative is "set in stone" unlike a human DM's responsive campaign
- Missing some 5e classes/subclasses/races vs. full rulebook
- No player-to-player romance (only player-to-NPC)
- Death reversible (spells, scrolls, save reload) — no permadeath by default

### Source 3: Deltia's Gaming — BG3 Reactions Explained
URL: https://deltiasgaming.com/baldurs-gate-3-reactions-explained/

- Reactions: special actions triggered by external events; limited to one per round per character
- Reaction toggle UI: Auto (fires automatically), Ask (interrupts for yes/no prompt), Off
- Opportunity Attack and Counterspell both compete for the single reaction slot per round
- The Ask mode is the digital equivalent of a DM calling "do you want to react?"

### Source 4: Wikipedia — Baldur's Gate 3
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baldur%27s_Gate_3

- Early Access: October 2020 (Act 1 only); Full release: August 3, 2023
- 7 awards at The Game Awards 2023 including GOTY; BAFTA Game of the Year 2024; Golden Joystick GOTY
- 20 million+ copies sold as of late 2025
- Peak concurrent Steam players: 800,000+ (record for CRPG)
- Built on Larian's Divinity Engine (same lineage as DOS2)

## Game 4: Disco Elysium (2019, ZA/UM)

### Source 1: Wikipedia — Disco Elysium
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disco_Elysium

- Release: October 15, 2019 (Windows); macOS April 27, 2020; Final Cut (full voice, consoles): March 30, 2021
- Developer/publisher: ZA/UM
- Engine: Unity
- 24 skills across 4 attributes (Intellect, Psyche, Physique, Motorics), 6 skills each
- Skills function as inner voices — speak directly to the player during checks and idle moments
- No traditional combat — all conflict resolved through skill checks and dialogue trees
- Metacritic: 91/100 original; PC Final Cut: 97/100
- Won Best Narrative, Best Indie, Best RPG at The Game Awards 2019
- Over 5 million copies sold as of September 2025
- Co-founders (Kurvitz, Bach, Rostov) departed ZA/UM by 2022 amid rights dispute

### Source 2: Disco Elysium devblog — Introducing the Thought Cabinet
URL: https://discoelysium.com/devblog/2019/09/30/introducing-the-thought-cabinet

- 53 total thoughts; players typically discover and internalize ~16 per playthrough
- Internalization takes 30 minutes to 3 in-game days in limited inventory slots
- Thoughts replace perk/trait/class/reputation systems simultaneously — "soul customization"
- Mechanical effects: bonuses and penalties tied to narrative themes (contemplating lost love expands visual range but corrodes character)
- Eight specialized thoughts: four Ideologies (alignment), four Copotypes (detective archetypes)
- Described as "loot for your mind, combining Fallout's trait system with Civilization's wonder mechanics"
- Thought Cabinet art by Anton Vill (concept artist, Mad Max: Fury Road)

### Source 3: Disco Elysium Fandom Wiki — Thought Cabinet
URL: https://discoelysium.fandom.com/wiki/Thought_Cabinet

- White orb appears when a thought "breaks through" after internalization period
- Spending skill points can permanently forget a completed thought (removing its benefits)
- Skills develop "notions" about the player based on actions, generating new thoughts organically

### Source 4: Reverse Shot — Righteous Skills
URL: https://reverseshot.org/features/2788/righteous_skills

- Academic/critical analysis of Disco Elysium's skill-as-voice design
- Skills alienate NPCs when too high — internal reputation system within the game

## Game 5: Divinity Original Sin 2 (2017, Larian Studios)

### Source 1: Wikipedia — Divinity: Original Sin II
URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divinity:_Original_Sin_II

- Release: September 14, 2017 (Windows); Definitive Edition 2018 (all platforms)
- Developer: Larian Studios
- Engine: Divinity Engine (proprietary; predecessor to BG3's engine)
- Physical and magical armor system: armor must be depleted before status effects (freeze/stun/knock-down) can apply
- Six origin characters: Lohse, Fane, Sebille, the Red Prince, Beast, Ifan ben-Mezd
- Races with unique abilities: elves can consume corpses for memories (cannibalism skill)
- 4-player co-op campaign; arena competitive mode
- Metacritic: original PC 93/100; Definitive Edition 95/100; GameSpot: 10/10
- Sales: 1 million copies by November 2017

### Source 2: PCWorld — DOS2 Game Master Mode
URL: https://www.pcworld.com/article/406706/divinity-original-sin-iis-game-master-mode-is-the-tabletop-rpg-sim-youve-been-waiting-for.html

- GM Mode: campaign creation tools, customizable maps (import images or pre-made Divinity maps), real-time GM control (add characters/items/monsters during play), dice rolling system, Vignettes (branching text scenes editable on-the-fly), Steam Workshop mod support
- Design goal: give GMs "visual infrastructure of the game engine while preserving tabletop improvisation"
- Example: player throws beehive at guards → GM rolls dice to resolve → no code needed for that action
- Positioned as superior to Sword Coast Legends (2015) for its richer GM flexibility

### Source 3: Gamepressure — Environmental Effects and Combinations
URL: https://www.gamepressure.com/originalsinii/environmental-effects-and-combinations/zea274

- Surface types: fire, water, ice, oil, blood, poison, electrified
- Sample reactions: water + lightning = electrified puddle; oil + fire = explosion then burning surface; blood + necromantic = additional mechanics
- Both player and enemy skills create surfaces; friendly fire is real
- Damage surface reactions: half the tick value on contact (e.g., fire tick 140 → 70 damage on ignition contact)

### Source 4: Fextralife — DOS2 Wiki
URL: https://divinityoriginalsin2.wiki.fextralife.com/Divinity+Original+Sin+2+Wiki

- Isometric, single-player or multiplayer
- Tactical turn-based combat; high-ground bonus system (elevation matters)
- Each origin character has a unique sidequest and unique dialogue options unavailable to others

### Source 5: Fextralife — GM Mode article
URL: https://fextralife.com/divinity-original-sin-2-game-master-mode/

- Full campaign creation tool in GM Mode
- Tabletop-style dice rolling system embedded in GM Mode
- Vignette tool for Choose Your Own Adventure flavor text moments
