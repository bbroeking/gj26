## Europa Universalis IV
- Source: eu4.paradoxwikis.com + gamesbeat.com review
- Paradox Development Studio, 2013, Clausewitz Engine
- Monarch power: three currencies (adm/dip/mil) spent to advance all state functions; perpetual scarcity creates trade-offs
- Trade nodes: directed graph; merchants steer trade toward home nodes; upstream nodes disproportionately valuable
- Institutions: historical forces spawn regionally and spread; adopting costs monarch points; ignoring them incurs tech-surcharges
- National Ideas: 8-slot per-nation tech tree shaped by historical identity
- 19+ DLC expansions layering mechanical modules (estates, ages, parliament) onto stable simulation core
- EU5 released November 2025

## Age of Empires II
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires_II + gamedeveloper.com meta-game balance article
- Ensemble Studios, 1999, Genie Engine; Definitive Edition 2019 (Forgotten Empires)
- Four-age advancement (Dark → Feudal → Castle → Imperial); building prerequisites gate age-ups
- Four resources: Food, Wood, Gold, Stone; market price volatility self-corrects imbalances with diminishing returns
- Rock-paper-scissors unit counter system across five military categories
- 13 original civs (now 40+ in DE); asymmetry via modifier bonuses + unique units within shared ruleset
- Shipped one year late; Genie Engine reused from AoE1; pathfinding a persistent weakness improved in DE
- 25+ years competitive lifespan; benchmark for asymmetric balance within shared ruleset

## Warcraft III
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos + en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_of_the_Ancients
- Blizzard Entertainment, 2002, proprietary 3D engine
- Hero units: XP → level 10 cap; spells + equippable items; RPG layer on top of RTS
- Creep camps: neutral hostiles guard map resources; killing them rewards XP + gold
- Item recipe system: combine base items into artifacts; economic decision layer
- Upkeep tax: food-based penalty at high army sizes (not a hard cap, a soft tax)
- World Editor: low barrier to modding; shipped with all copies; Battle.net map sharing
- DotA genesis: "Eul" built DoTA using World Editor 2003; "Guinsoo" (Steve Feak) added recipe items + Roshan boss; "IceFrog" stewarded long-term; 1.5M+ registered forum users by 2009
- Valve acquired DotA IP 2009 → Dota 2 (2013); Riot Games cited DotA as League of Legends inspiration

## Total War Warhammer III
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_War:_Warhammer_III + totalwarwarhammer.fandom.com + nme.com interview
- Creative Assembly / Sega, February 17 2022, Warscape Engine
- Dual-layer: turn-based campaign + real-time battles (auto-resolve makes real-time optional)
- Seven launch factions with asymmetric campaign mechanics (not just stat changes)
- Realm of Chaos: structured mid-campaign gauntlet — four daemon realm invasions with survival battle boss encounters
- Daemon Prince faction: collect boss soul trophies as physical body-part upgrades (trophy → character upgrade loop)
- Immortal Empires: post-launch free update merging all three Warhammer games; 295 start positions
- Outpost system: build in ally territory, recruit from their roster
- Metacritic 86/100; 2.34M units sold by March 2025

## Humankind
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humankind_(video_game) + ancientworldmagazine.com review
- Amplitude Studios / Sega, August 2021, GAMES2 Engine
- Culture-swapping: choose 1 of 10 cultures per era (7 eras, 60 total); each grants unique unit + Legacy Trait that persists
- Single Fame victory condition (vs Civilization's multiple parallel tracks)
- FIMS + Influence economy; Influence as soft power currency
- Tactical battles: sub-map, 3-round limit before auto-resolve; terrain advantages active
- Neolithic opening before classical era begins
- Removed Denuvo post-launch after performance benchmarks
- Metacritic 77/100 (Windows); Aug 2021 4th best-selling PC game

## Battletech 2018
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BattleTech_(video_game) + sarna.net wiki + store.steampowered.com
- Harebrained Schemes / Paradox Interactive, April 24 2018, Unity engine
- Jordan Weisman (original tabletop creator) directing
- Lance of 4 mechs; turn order by initiative (speed/size); hex-like terrain grid
- Called shots: only available when enemy mech knocked down; target specific location → high reward
- Heat management: all weapons generate heat; exceeding cap risks shutdown / ammo explosion / pilot injury
- Mech customization: chassis, weapons, armor, actuators, gyros each adjustable in Mech Bay
- Mercenary economy: start in debt, missions pay C-Bills minus repair costs; skull-rating risk assessment
- Pilot injury system: repeated injury risks permanent stat loss or death; morale buffs defensive actions
- Funded Kickstarter 2015 ($1.85M); procedural contract sandbox unlocked as stretch goal
- Tabletop damage × 5 for digital translation
- Metacritic 80/100; three DLC expansions (Flashpoint, Urban Warfare, Heavy Metal)

## Endless Legend
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endless_Legend + 4xmechanics.wordpress.com + finalboss.io interview
- Amplitude Studios / Iceberg Interactive, September 2014, GAMES2 Engine
- 14 factions (8 at launch); each plays by different campaign rules (not just stat bonuses)
  - Broken Lords: pay Dust instead of Food to sustain population
  - Necrophages: cannot sign peace treaties; forced aggression
  - Cultists: no cities; convert independent villages instead
- FIDS economy: Food, Industry, Dust, Science; Dust = multi-role (income + hero power + diplomacy)
- One city per region (hard expansion density cap); tall vs wide tension
- Winter mechanic: seasonal attrition escalating each game year; winter-adapted factions advantaged
- Faction quest chains: multi-stage narrative + mechanical reward; alternate victory path
- Hero units with skill trees + crafted equipment
- RPS GOTY 2014; Metacritic 82/100; Endless Legend 2 announced (Hooded Horse, 2025)

## Old World
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_World_(video_game) + mohawkgames.com + metacritic
- Mohawk Games (Soren Johnson, ex-Civ IV lead designer), July 1 2021, Unity engine
- Epic Games Store early access 2020; Steam release July 2021
- Orders system: limited pool of action points per turn shared across all units and cities; forces prioritization
- Dynasty and succession: leaders age and die; cultivate heirs, arrange marriages; incompetent heir reverses decades of progress
- Character traits: emerge from life events + player choices; events are scripted + semi-procedural
- Ambitions: medium-term pacing goals to prevent mid-game drift
- Focused scope: classical antiquity only (not 6,000 years); enables deeper thematic coherence
- Civics yield: separate cultural production → government/law unlocks
- Soren Johnson design blog (Wayward Thinker) documents design intent
- PC Gamer Best Strategy Game 2021; Metacritic 80/100

## Terra Nil
- Source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terra_Nil + sciencefriday.com + gamereview.monster + taptap.io
- Free Lives (South Africa) / Devolver Digital, 2023; also Netflix Games (mobile)
- Unity engine; tile-based grid; four biome regions (Temperate, Tropical, Polar, Continental)
- Three-phase structure: (1) Terraform → (2) Biome-ify → (3) Deconstruct + depart
- Area-based spatial puzzle logic; biome adjacency requirements
- Biome percentage thresholds as win conditions per map
- No economy, no currency, no resources; spatial constraint only
- Ecological interdependence: some biomes require adjacent prerequisite biomes
- Relaxed tone; no failure state in first two phases
- Metacritic 79/100 (PC), 84/100 (iOS); Nominated Best Mobile Game TGA 2023

## Cross-cutting notes
- EU4 + AoE2 + Old World all use scarcity/prioritization as primary design tension
- WC3 + TW:WH3 + Battletech all have trophy/boss-reward systems analogous to Wayfinder's boss chain
- Endless Legend + Humankind both from Amplitude; GAMES2 Engine; culture/faction identity as replay driver
- Terra Nil is the cozy/ecological closest in tone to Wayfinder; deconstruction phase maps to "return from dungeon" beat
- WC3 World Editor → DotA → MOBA is the canonical "platform beats product" case study; see also [[Dota 2]] [[League of Legends]]
