## New World (Amazon Games, 2021)

**Sources fetched:**
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_World_(video_game) — release date Sep 2021, developer Amazon Games OC, Amazon Lumberyard engine, three factions, crafting trade skill categories, Steam launch (700K concurrent, 5th most-played ever), Metacritic 70, console relaunch Oct 2024 as "Aeternum", shutdown Jan 31 2027
- https://gamerant.com/new-world-how-to-get-find-farm-use-azoth-guide/ — Azoth mechanics: base 50 fast-travel cost, distance/encumbrance/faction multipliers, crafting use (affix/gem-slot chance), mastery respec use
- https://gamerant.com/new-world-azoth-cap-travel-costs/ — player criticism: cap too low, fast-travel so costly players died instead of paying, community demand for cap increase
- https://www.mmorpg.com/news/new-world-team-talks-q2-economy-inflation-whats-working-and-changes-to-come-2000131269 — developer Q2 economy update: inflation from supply exploits, orichalcum abundance adjustment, team's view of what sinks were and weren't working
- https://kotaku.com/new-world-players-face-massive-queues-wait-times-on-ov-1847763621 — launch queue crisis: 700K+ day-one players, 212-hour queue times, emergency server expansion

**Additional search data:**
- Launch peak 913,000 concurrent Steam players; current population under 4% of peak
- RTX 3090 bricking incident (uncapped fps in menus during beta)
- Faction imbalance: no balancing mechanics to prevent everyone joining dominant faction
- Territory tax/fee control by governing companies; settlement upgrade paths (economy vs. war)
