## Potion Craft: Alchemist Simulator (niceplay games / tinyBuild, 1.0 Dec 2022)

Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potion_Craft
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/1210320/Potion_Craft_Alchemist_Simulator/
- https://www.wycoconutgaming.com/potion-craft-alchemist-simulator-review/
- https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/VideoGame/PotionCraft
- https://potion-craft.fandom.com/wiki/Potion_Craft_Wiki

Key facts extracted:
- Developer: niceplay games (Mikhail Chuprakov, Russian indie); Publisher: tinyBuild
- Engine: Unity
- Release: EA Sep 21 2021 → 1.0 Dec 2022 → console Dec 2023 → 2.0 Dec 2024
- Core mechanic: grind ingredients in mortar → navigate alchemy map (each ingredient plots a trajectory) → heat cauldron at target coordinates → potion created
- Map hazards: bones, vortexes degrade quality; rare crystals from dwarves help navigate
- Mortar: fine motor precision required; over-grinding wastes reagents
- Recipe book: player records discovered recipes for quick reproduction
- Shop loop: haggle with customers, barter with traders, quality tiers I–II–III set price ceilings, reputation affects foot traffic
- Inspiration: Kingdom Come: Deliverance potion minigame + developer's prior alchemy prototypes
- Awards: 2023 IGF honorable mentions (Design + Visual Arts); D.I.C.E. Strategy/Sim GOTY nominee; Steam "Sit Back and Relax" nominations 2021 + 2023
- Quote (RPS): "the best crafting system ever concocted"
- Quote (PC Gamer): "captures the magic of invention and creation in a way more 'realistic' crafting systems often don't"

- https://www.invioxstudios.com/blog/how-solo-dev-dan-beckerton-took-spirittea-from-kickstarter-to-game-pass

Key facts extracted:
- Developer: Dan Beckerton (Cheesemaster Games), solo; Publisher: No More Robots
- Engine: GameMaker Studio 2 (switched from Unity early; prior GMS2 experience from Fables of Talumos 2019)
- Release: Nov 13, 2023 (PC + Switch + Xbox); day-one Game Pass
- Backstory: Kickstarter Jan 2020 (4 months into dev), living off savings in Vancouver; campaign nearly failed, succeeded via high-tier pledges
- Core loops: (1) bathhouse management — chop wood, heat baths, wash towels, seat spirits with friends, serve food; (2) spirit hunting — investigate hauntings with clues, witness transformation, recruit patron; (3) NPC relationships — 24 townspeople with routines, hobbies, shared activities
- Player controls sleep (not calendar-locked); "end goal" = finish aspiring author's book (optional social depth)
- Spirit-seating mechanic: spirit compatibility affects satisfaction; Pokémon-esque collection element
- Inspirations: Spirited Away (bathhouse), Natsume's Book of Friends (spirit encounters), Barakamon (rural relocation), Earthbound + Harvest Moon (pixel art aesthetic)
- Playtime: 40–60 hrs full playthrough; 20–30 hrs to see all spirits
- Reviews: 7/10 Nintendo Life; 8/10 Screen Rant; OpenCritic generally positive
- Criticism: some minigames described as "unnecessarily convoluted"; fiddly UI; performance stuttering noted

## Dinkum (James Bendon / Krafton, 1.0 April 2025)

Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinkum_(video_game)
- https://www.capsulecomputers.com.au/2022/07/dinkum-preview/
- https://www.digitaltq.com/dinkum-licences-guide
- https://www.digitaltq.com/dinkum-permit-points
- https://techraptor.net/gaming/guides/dinkum-building-guide
- https://www.whatsitlike.com.au/interview-with-dinkum-developer-james-bendon/

Key facts extracted:
- Developer: James Bendon (Queensland, Australia), solo; Publisher: Krafton
- Engine: Unity
- Release: EA July 2022 → 1.0 April 23 2025 → Switch Nov 6 2025; 8-year development (started Nov 2017)
- Dev story: taught himself 3D modelling and programming; quit retail job; 1M+ units by Sep 2025
- Title: "fair dinkum" = Australian slang for genuine/authentic
- Setting: randomly generated island, 4 biomes (bushland, tropical, desert, cold); Australian outback flora/fauna
- Core activities: farming, mining, fishing, bug-catching, town-building
- Permit Points: secondary currency earned via milestones (distance walked, bugs caught, museum donations, bulletin board tasks, NPC daily jobs, Dinks conversion at bank); used to buy Licenses from NPC Fletch
- Licenses: unlock tool tiers and crafting categories (skill-tree analogue)
- Town-building: Deed → place → supply construction materials → pay Debt; one Deed at a time forces sequential town growth
- Design philosophy: avoid "video gamey" abstractions; shop items on world tables not menus; legible NPC movement
- Energy bar: depletes with actions, restored by food/sleep; forces daily prioritization
- Criticism: lack of Indigenous Australian representation despite strong Australian setting
- Commercial: 1M+ units by Sep 2025; generally positive reviews
