## Angband

Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angband_(video_game)
- http://angband-dev.blogspot.com/2011/09/so-what-exactly-is-ego-item-anyway.html
- http://www.phial.com/angband/angfaq2.html
- https://www.roguebasin.com/index.php/Angband (403 — fallback to search results)

Key facts extracted:
- Created ~1990 (Alex Cutler, Andy Astrand, Univ. of Warwick); public release April 11, 1993 (v2.4); current v4.2.6 (Dec 2025)
- 100-floor descent to defeat Morgoth; permadeath enforced on save
- Non-persistent levels: leaving and returning regenerates the floor (monsters, loot, layout); makes dungeon effectively infinite; anti-farm by architecture
- Item tiers: plain → ego items (typed prefix/suffix, e.g., Scimitar of Frost) → fixed artifacts (one per game, static list); most look like junk until identified
- Town level at depth 0: shops, safe hub, gold economy
- Variant ecosystem: MAngband (multiplayer, 1997), Zangband, ToME (2012)
- David Brevik cited Angband as "huge influence" and "the model" for Diablo's randomized levels and items
- Ben Harrison "Great Code Cleanup" modularized codebase into reusable engine

Wiki page: [[Angband]]

## Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup

Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Crawl_Stone_Soup
- https://libregamewiki.org/Dungeon_Crawl_Stone_Soup
- https://orbitalcrypt.blogspot.com/2022/03/small-gods-and-stone-soup-deities-made.html
- https://crawl.develz.org/wiki/doku.php?id=dcss:brainstorm:god:concept:general

Key facts extracted:
- Fork of Linley's Dungeon Crawl (1997); DCSS launched Sept 19, 2006; current stable 0.34.1
- Explicit anti-grind doctrine: food system removed, magic schools pruned, "featuritis" avoided
- 27 species × 26 backgrounds; species effect > background effect on playstyle
- 26 gods with distinct mechanical contracts (Okawaru for fighters, Sif Muna for mages, Jiyva for mutation); god balance actively maintained
- Win condition: collect 3 of 15 runes → enter Zot → retrieve Orb of Zot (forces branch choice)
- Procgen floors with hand-crafted vault injections
- Web/Android/desktop; GNU GPL-2.0-or-later; biannual tournaments on public servers; ghost encounters

Wiki page: [[Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup]]

Key facts extracted:
- Developer: Housemarque; Publisher: Sony Interactive Entertainment; PS5 Apr 30 2021; PC (Climax Studios) Feb 15 2023
- Engine: Unreal Engine 4; DualSense haptics, Tempest 3D audio, ray tracing; ~1080p + temporal upscale to 4K/60fps
- 6 biomes in 2 acts of 3; death resets to act start; boss defeats permanently open portals (run-start skip)
- Planet "changes with every loop" — procedurally varied rooms/enemies/items per run while biome identity persists
- 10 weapon types with upgradeable traits (traits persist as unlocks across runs); weapons lost on death
- Parasites: equippable alien creatures with buff + linked debuff (risk-contract item type)
- Narrative: protagonist Selene Vassos's time-loop IS the story; permadeath is canonical repetition
- Won BAFTA Games Award for Best Game; Sony acquired Housemarque June 2021

## Against the Storm

Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Against_the_Storm_(video_game)
- https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-against-the-storm-managed-to-mix-city-building-and-roguelite-play
- https://techraptor.net/gaming/reviews/against-storm-ambient-roguelite-city-builder
- https://gamermatters.com/against-the-storm-steam-early-access-impressions-...

Key facts extracted:
- Developer: Eremite Games; Publisher: Hooded Horse; EA Oct 2021; full release Dec 8 2023; console Jun 2025
- Engine: Unity; 91/100 Metacritic; 1M+ copies by May 2024; won Best Design at Taipei Game Show 2024
- Key design insight: "the city is your avatar" (designer Michał Ogłoziński) → roguelite verbs (quests, health, leveling) apply naturally
- 7 species (Humans, Beavers, Harpies, Lizards, Foxes + DLC Frogs, Bats); 3 per village with species-specific needs
- 3 victory paths: Orders (Queen tasks), Resolve (settler happiness), Exploration (glade resources)
- Cornerstone perks at season transitions = run's affix layer; 3 seasons per run with shifting buffs/debuffs
- Meta-progression: Smoldering City tech tree (production rates, settler speed, building unlocks)
- Stability boredom solved: run concludes before settlement becomes trivial; Queen as narrative timer device
- Blightrot/Corruption mechanic at higher difficulties (pollution consequences)
- ~1 hour per run

Wiki page: [[Against the Storm]]

- ~30-min sessions; free demo available
- Compared to Slay the Spire and Balatro in player reception

## Wildfrost

Sources fetched:
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wildfrost
- https://screenrant.com/wildfrost-pc-review/
- https://chucklefish.org/blog/announcing-wildfrost/
- https://wildfrostwiki.com/Wildfrost_Wiki (landing page only; subpages not fetched)
- https://www.escapistmagazine.com/wildfrost-review-in-3-minutes/

Key facts extracted:
- Developer: Deadpan Games + Gaziter; Publisher: Chucklefish; Engine: Unity
- Switch + Windows Apr 12 2023; Android/iOS Apr 11 2024; Xbox Dec 10 2024
- Metacritic 80–87 depending on platform; iOS highest aggregate
- Counter system: replaces energy with countdown timers on all cards; when counter hits 0, card acts
- 6-slot battlefield (3 player / 3 enemy); free repositioning between turns; positional depth
- 3 tribes: Snowdwellers (balanced), Shademancers (sacrifice), Clunkmasters (constructs)
- Leaders randomly selected from chosen tribe; tribe shapes card pool
- Charms: attachable relics that modify individual cards/companions (primary run customization layer)
- Companions = named individuals with persistent identity, not generic card instances
- Injured cards shuffle back for free healing
- Snowdwell hub: last surviving settlement; achievement-gated unlocks add companions/charms to future pools
- Frost Guardian + Storm Bells = difficulty customization

Wiki page: [[Wildfrost]]
