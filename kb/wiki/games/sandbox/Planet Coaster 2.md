# Planet Coaster 2

A theme-park management and creation sim by Frontier Developments (November 2024, PC/PS5/Xbox Series) that layers an expressive piece-by-piece coaster builder, terrain sculpting, and an event sequencer on top of a park-management economy, with cross-platform co-op and a community Blueprint Workshop holding 70,000+ shared creations.

## Design

**Creation tools.** The coaster builder is the centrepiece: players lay track one segment at a time, with excitement ratings driven by G-force, inversions, speed variation, and "head chopper" near-miss moments. Pathing switched in PC2 from piece-by-piece to a **draw system** for faster layout. Terrain tools offer sculpt, flatten, chisel, and paint brushes with controllable size and intensity for hills, lakes, and caves. An **Event Sequencer** groups objects into a timeline, triggering animations and effects — essentially a simple keyframe editor for show scenes and fireworks. **Blueprints** save arbitrary groups of objects (a single queue entrance, a full themed land, an entire coaster) for instant re-use.

**Discoverability.** The **Frontier Workshop** hosts over 70,000 community blueprints, browsable by theme, coaster type, or share-code search. On PC, Steam Workshop integration complements the proprietary portal. A 2025 Update 3 added Featured Creators lists, follower alerts for new uploads, and a "What's Hot" leaderboard — the first algorithmic discovery layer on top of the pure browse interface.

**Social.** **Franchise Mode** is the live multiplayer layer: players visit or collaborate on each other's parks online, with shared saves enabling asynchronous co-building. Sandbox Mode supports unlimited creative freedom without management pressure. Career Mode introduces scripted goals that teach systems before players move to Sandbox.

**Standout mechanic.** The **Event Sequencer** bridges sandbox creativity and authored entertainment: a ride reveal with pyrotechnics, a haunted-house scare sequence, or a themed parade all use the same tool, making show design feel as tangible as coaster building.

## Implementation

Published by **Frontier Developments** on **Windows (Steam), PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X/S**, released **November 6, 2024**. Cross-platform play is supported in Franchise Mode. Management systems cover guest satisfaction, staff operations, financial metrics, and heat-map analytics. Metacritic score: 76/100 (critics praised creation depth; management was widely criticised as undersized relative to the building suite). Update 2 (February 2025) added customisable video billboards and synchronised ride launches.

## Why it matters

Planet Coaster 2 demonstrates that a deep creation toolset and a thin but functional Workshop can coexist in a single-purchase game — no subscription, no creator marketplace, no user-economy. The 70,000+ blueprint library grew organically from player passion, not monetary incentive. The 2025 roadmap additions (Featured Creators, What's Hot) show a studio layering discovery incrementally post-launch. The gap between the builder's sophistication and the management layer's shallowness is instructive: in hybrid create/manage games, the two halves must scale together or one cannibalises the other's reputation.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

1. **[[Economy]]** — Blueprint sharing without a creator economy (no tokens, no splits) is an alternative to monetary UGC models; relevant when deciding whether Wayfinder chart-sharing should be free exchange or traded commodity.
2. **[[UI Workflow]]** — The Event Sequencer's timeline-trigger model is a reference for any Wayfinder encounter-scripting or dungeon-event system (boss phase triggers, puzzle reveals, trap sequences).
3. **[[Multiplayer Co-op]]** — Franchise Mode's shared-save co-building (asynchronous) vs. same-session co-play is a useful distinction when scoping Wayfinder co-op dungeon charting.
