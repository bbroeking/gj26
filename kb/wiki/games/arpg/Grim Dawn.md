# Grim Dawn

Dark fantasy ARPG released February 2016 by Crate Entertainment (built on the Titan Quest engine) featuring a dual mastery class system, a sprawling devotion constellation tree, NPC faction allegiances, and item skill modifiers that let gear reshape class abilities.

## Design

**Dual mastery system.** Players choose one of six base masteries (Soldier, Demolitionist, Occultist, Nightblade, Arcanist, Shaman) at the start; a second mastery unlocks at level 10. Two expansions added three more (Inquisitor, Necromancer, Oathkeeper), bringing total combinations to 66. Each combination has a named class identity (Soldier + Demolitionist = Commando; Soldier + Necromancer = Death Knight). Skill trees from both masteries are fully accessible simultaneously, with an additional mastery attribute bar that provides passive bonuses as points are invested.

**Devotion constellation system.** A large star-map of constellation clusters, unlocked by finding and restoring corrupted shrines across the world. Players spend devotion points to claim constellation nodes — passive bonuses and, notably, powerful secondary effects (procs, buffs, summons) that can be bound to any class skill. This creates a third build axis independent of class and gear.

**Faction system.** Seventeen NPC factions offer reputation tracks through questing and enemy kills. Higher reputation unlocks faction vendors, crafting blueprints, augments, and item discounts; some factions are mutually antagonistic (siding with one locks out another). This adds a choice-and-consequence layer to the ARPG loop rare in the genre.

**Itemization and item skill modifiers.** Standard rarity tiers (Normal → Magic → Rare → Epic → Legendary) with component-crafting: salvaged components can be assembled into unique pieces via blueprints. The defining late-design feature is **item skill modifiers**: expansion-era Legendary and Epic items carry passive lines that directly augment specific class skills — increasing a skill's damage type, adding projectiles, or converting its damage element. This makes specific item hunts revolve around build enablers rather than raw stat upgrades.

**Endgame.** Shattered Realm (endless wave dungeon with shard progression), Crucible (wave arena), and Forgotten Gods' Lokarr's Secret Quest. No seasonal/league system; content is permanent.

## Implementation

Built on the proprietary Titan Quest engine (C++), acquired and modified by Crate. Windows release February 2016 via Steam Early Access (started January 2013); Xbox One port December 2021. Three paid expansions: Ashes of Malmouth (2017), The Forgotten Gods (2019), Fangs of Asterkarn (2024). By February 2022: 7 million units sold across game and DLC. Metacritic 83/100 (PC), 86/100 (Xbox).

## Why it matters

Grim Dawn demonstrates that the dual mastery concept (pioneered by [[Titan Quest]]) scales to enormous depth when combined with a third system (devotion) and item-level skill modification. The faction system is one of the genre's few attempts to make the game world feel politically alive rather than a backdrop for killing. Item skill modifiers are a model for how itemization can gate build archetypes without being pure stat-inflation.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **[[Skills]] via item modifiers.** Grim Dawn's item skill modifier pattern — gear that reshapes a class ability rather than merely buffing its number — is applicable to Wayfinder's [[Items and Gear]] design: a found item could alter how a [[Skills|hotbar skill]] behaves, creating hunt targets around build enablers rather than stat ceilings.
- **[[Affixes]] and build identity.** The dual mastery + devotion stack shows how layered systems create combinatorial build space. Wayfinder's [[Affixes]] on [[Charts]] could achieve similar depth if chart affixes interact meaningfully with Trade skill choices.
- **Faction alignment and [[Chart Loop]].** The faction reputation model — allegiance shapes available rewards and unlocks — could map to how different chart types (biomes, bosses) offer faction-flavored unlocks in Wayfinder's [[Chart Loop]].
