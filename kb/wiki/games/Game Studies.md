# Game Studies

A library of **game case studies** — one page per title covering its **design**
(core loop, progression, economy, social, the standout mechanic) and
**implementation** (engine/tech, netcode/architecture where notable), with a
short **Relevance to Wayfinder** note. The point is breadth: a cross-genre bank
of "how others did it" to pull from when designing [[Wayfinding|Wayfinder]].

- **Catalog + progress:** [[Game Index]] (target: **260** titles, grouped by genre).
- **Companion research:** [[MMO Research]] (deep MMO design + implementation) and
  its synthesis [[MMO Lessons for Wayfinder]]; plus [[Design Influences]] (what
  Wayfinder already borrows).

Pages live in `wiki/games/<genre>/`; web-research provenance in `raw/games/`.
Each page is a focused case study (~250–500 words), not a manual — it captures
the transferable idea and links to the cross-cutting design pages
([[MMO Economy and Itemization]], [[MMO Progression Systems]],
[[MMO Netcode and Tick Systems]], [[MMO Social and Endgame]]) and the Wayfinder
systems it speaks to.
