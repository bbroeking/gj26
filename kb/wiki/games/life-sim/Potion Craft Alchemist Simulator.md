# Potion Craft Alchemist Simulator

Alchemy crafting sim (EA 2021 / 1.0 December 2022 / 2.0 December 2024, niceplay games / tinyBuild) — a Russian indie studio built a potion shop where every brew is a physical navigation puzzle on a map-space, earning Rock Paper Shotgun's label "the best crafting system ever concocted."

## Design

- **Physical map-based brewing:** The central mechanic replaces recipe look-ups with spatial navigation. Each ingredient, once ground in a mortar and pestle, moves a cursor along a specific trajectory on an alchemy map; heating the cauldron at certain coordinates converts the brew into a potion with those effects. Players must chart a course across the map to reach desired effect nodes while avoiding hazards (bones, vortexes) that degrade quality.
- **Mortar grinding as motor-control skill:** Grinding intensity is a fine-tuned variable — over-grinding wastes reagents, under-grinding doesn't move the cursor far enough. The mechanic rewards precision without demanding reflexes, sitting in the cozy-skill zone.
- **Discovery and recipe book:** The map starts obscured; experimentation reveals it. Recipes the player invents are recorded in a personal book for quick reproduction. This discovery-first design means every player's knowledge is earned rather than looked up.
- **Shop economy and customer negotiation:** Players haggle with customers (setting prices, accepting or countering offers) and barter with ingredient traders to lower costs. Reputation affects foot traffic; potion quality tiers (I–III) set price ceilings. The loop is: brew → sell → reinvest in rarer ingredients → access deeper map regions.
- **Inspiration:** Developer Mikhail Chuprakov cited the potion-brewing minigame in Kingdom Come: Deliverance (2018) and his own prior alchemy prototypes as seed ideas; the map-navigation approach emerged as the way to make every ingredient feel physically meaningful.

## Implementation

- **Engine:** Unity.
- **Studio:** niceplay games — a small Russian independent studio founded by Chuprakov. Published by tinyBuild.
- **Release arc:** Early Access September 2021 → 1.0 December 2022 → console ports (Switch, PS4/5) December 2023 → major 2.0 update December 2024. The phased release allowed the economy and haggling systems to be redesigned based on feedback before 1.0.
- **Reception:** 2023 Independent Games Festival honorable mentions for Excellence in Design and Visual Arts; nominated for D.I.C.E. Strategy/Simulation Game of the Year; multiple Steam "Sit Back and Relax" award nominations. PC Gamer called it "a clever, abstract alchemy sim that captures the magic of invention and creation in a way more 'realistic' crafting systems often don't."

## Why it matters

Potion Craft proves that **the crafting act itself can be the game** — not a menu-driven intermediary, but a tactile puzzle whose solution space the player gradually maps. The mortar-grind + map-navigate system turns ingredient selection into strategic path-planning and makes every new ingredient a genuine discovery event. It also shows that shop economics (reputation, haggling, quality tiers) can layer meaningful mid-game decisions onto an otherwise meditative loop without tipping into stress.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **Physical crafting as precedent for Ink inscription:** Potion Craft's mortar-navigate system is the closest published analogue to Wayfinder's chart inscription mechanic — [[Inks]] applied in sequence could trace paths through an Affix space the way reagents trace paths through the potion map; see [[The Crafting Bench]] and [[Inks]] for the direct design thread.
- **Discovery-before-recipe:** The obscured map and personal recipe book model a knowledge progression Wayfinder [[Crafting]] could adopt — players earn recipes by experimenting with material combinations rather than unlocking them from a static tree.
- **Economy tier loop:** Quality tiers driving price ceilings is a clean template for how [[Economy]] should tier crafted goods — common charts at low sell values, master-inscribed charts worth significantly more to the right [[NPCs]].
