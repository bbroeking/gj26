# Sun Haven

A 2023 cozy fantasy farm-sim / action RPG by Pixel Sprout Studios (Unity; LA indie team), released to Steam Early Access June 2021 and full release March 2023, distinguishing itself from Stardew Valley via three fantasy biomes, a class-based RPG combat system, no stamina mechanic, and 8-player co-op.

## Design

- **Core loop:** farm in the medieval town of Sun Haven, mine, fish, and cook — then push into adjacent fantasy biomes that escalate combat and resource demands. The absence of a stamina bar is the headline feature: players can act indefinitely in a day, removing the classic pacing gate that defines Stardew/Harvest Moon rhythm.
- **World and biomes:** four distinct areas — Sun Haven (medieval human town), Nel'Vari (elven forest), Withergate (monster city), The Brinestone Deeps (underwater, added mid-2024). Each region has unique crops, crafting recipes, and enemies. Players maintain up to 3 separate farms with biome-specific crops.
- **RPG class system:** 10 starting professions (analogous to D&D archetypes) and 7 playable races. Combat is real-time action. Pacifist routes exist for boss encounters and story moments, allowing players to avoid fighting entirely.
- **Skill trees:** five disciplines — combat, exploration, farming, fishing, mining — each capped at level 70. Skill elixirs and craftable skill tomes allow targeted advancement boosts.
- **Crafting depth:** 40+ unique crafting tables spanning cooking, smithing, construction, farming, furniture, and alchemy. Over 130 fish species. Over 30,000 lines of dialogue. Fantasy creatures as farmable animals: silk moths, baby griffons, phoenixes, werewolves, tar slimes.
- **Magic:** spells available in both combat and farming contexts (crop-watering spells, offensive combat magic) — magic is a cross-domain tool rather than siloed to one activity.
- **Multiplayer co-op:** up to 8 players share a single farm; characters retain individual inventories and XP across single-player and multiplayer sessions. Switch version (2024) launched without multiplayer.
- **NPC relationships:** 30+ romanceable/friendable NPCs including Lucia, Sun Haven's mayor; over 30,000 dialogue lines support character depth.

## Implementation

- **Engine:** Unity (pixel art rendering).
- **Kickstarter origin:** Pixel Sprout Studios (LA-based indie) launched a Kickstarter in 2020; after successful funding, opened to additional crowdfunding via IndieGoGo due to community demand, then Steam Early Access June 2021.
- **Early Access development:** roughly 2 years of public Early Access before full 1.0 release March 11, 2023 — the open EA period served as a live design testbed and community feedback loop.
- **Switch port:** released November 2024 (Americas) and December 2024 (Europe) — a 20-month gap from PC 1.0 to Switch, with multiplayer absent from the console version at launch.
- **Stamina removal:** a deliberate design differentiator from launch — removing the energy gate shifts the daily loop from "ration your actions carefully" to "do everything, pace yourself by season deadline" — a meaningful change in how time pressure functions.

## Why it matters

- The stamina-free design is the clearest test of what happens when you remove the single most universal constraint in the farming-sim genre: activity becomes more freeform but the day can feel less structured, requiring alternative pacing architecture (season deadlines, NPC event triggers).
- Five parallel skill trees with level-70 ceilings and 4 distinct biomes represent the ceiling-case for "how much content can a farming sim add before it stops feeling cozy?" — a useful calibration point for scope.
- 8-player co-op with shared farm + individual XP retention is the most generous cozy co-op design found in the genre; the shared farm model shows both the appeal (collaborative building) and the tension (whose crop is whose?).

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **[[Trades and Leveling]]:** five parallel skill trees that reach level 70 per discipline, all progressing simultaneously during normal play, is the closest analogue to Wayfinder's multi-Trade leveling structure. The alchemy table as a cross-skill crafting surface mirrors how [[Crafting]] in Wayfinder draws on multiple Trade outputs.
- **[[Gathering]]:** biome-gated resources (Nel'Vari elven crops, Withergate monster drops) mirror Wayfinder's chart affixes that gate specific resource types behind dungeon difficulty — both use world geography as a progression gate for material tiers.
- **[[Multiplayer Co-op]]:** shared farm with individual XP retention is one solution to the "who owns the resource?" co-op problem; compare to Palia's per-player loot drop approach — Sun Haven's model incentivizes base-building collaboration while Palia's avoids ownership conflicts entirely.
