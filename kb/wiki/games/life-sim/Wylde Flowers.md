# Wylde Flowers

A 2022 cozy farming sim / mystery narrative by Melbourne studio Studio Drydock (Unity; ~50-person team), launched as an Apple Arcade exclusive before coming to PC and Switch, notable for weaving a witch-coven storyline and LGBTQ+ inclusive romance into a standard seasonal farming loop.

## Design

- **Core loop:** seasonal farming in the seaside town of Fairhaven — planting, harvesting, and cooking (e.g., mushroom risotto for Grandma) — serves as the comfortable, habitual spine that grounds the escalating mystery narrative.
- **Witchcraft system:** protagonist Tara discovers her late grandmother's powers, joins a secret coven, and learns spells drawn from international mythology (Russian, South African, Iranian traditions). A cat transformation mechanic recurs throughout. Magic serves narrative purposes — spells resolve plot beats (e.g., compelling wraiths) — rather than acting as a combat or resource-production tool.
- **Slow-boil narrative structure:** the tone is explicitly designed as a "slow-boiling frog" — warm cozy welcome that gradually introduces mystery and supernatural threat. The team's daily design question was "Was it dark enough? Was it cozy enough? Did the mystery unfold at the right cadence?"
- **Relationship system:** 7 potential romance paths across multiple genders; 30 named townsfolk with individual depth; fully voiced with 18 hours of dialogue across 350 cutscenes. LGBTQ+ characters (including non-binary Kim, voiced by Erika Ishii) are written as whole humans, not defined by their queerness.
- **Player customization:** limited to clothing and hair; character identity is fixed as Tara. Day-length is adjustable for self-pacing. No combat.
- **Seasonal structure:** standard four-season cycle; farming rhythm anchors the player between narrative chapters.

## Implementation

- **Engine:** Unity; shared build for iterative testing, with daily tweaks across a distributed team.
- **Camera:** shipped as angled isometric, zooming progressively closer to characters during dialogue — a conscious shift from an initial top-down mobile-optimized design to emphasize acting and emotional connection.
- **Team scale:** started at ~12, expanded fourfold over 3 years; final credits list 230 names including an orchestral score. Studio Drydock co-founded by Amanda Schofield (prior: Senior Producer on Sims FreePlay) and Alex Holkner.
- **Voice production:** actors improvised lines; writers rewrote on Slack during recording sessions. Cultural consultants vetted dialects, settings, and dialogue for the game's international mythology references.
- **Platform strategy:** Apple Arcade exclusive at launch (February 2022) — avoiding the Steam crowding problem and ensuring a polished, subscription-funded release; PC and Switch followed September 2022.
- **Awards:** 2022 Apple Design Award; Gayming Awards "Best LGBTQ+ Indie Game."
- **Sequel:** *Wylde Society* announced December 2024 — a prequel set at the turn of the 20th century, following Vivian Wylde managing a boarding house.

## Why it matters

- Demonstrates that a non-combat cozy game can sustain 18+ hours of engagement by layering authored mystery narrative over a farming spine — the mystery gives players a "pull" goal that daily farming tasks do not provide on their own.
- The "slow-boil frog" tone model (cozy entry → escalating stakes) is a concrete technique for pacing a game that needs to feel safe and exciting simultaneously.
- Schofield's Sims background informs a key insight: players love authoring their own stories, but that doesn't mean they don't want handcrafted narrative moments — the two coexist and each makes the other feel richer.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **[[NPCs]]:** the 30-character voiced ensemble with individual questlines and romance paths is a ceiling-case model for NPC depth; Wylde Flowers shows how much narrative investment is needed to make relationship systems feel alive at ~18-hour scale.
- **[[Crafting]]:** farming → cooking pipeline as the comfort spine that persists between high-stakes narrative beats mirrors how Wayfinder's cozy skilling (gather → craft → cook) should feel like a safe home base between chart delves.
- **[[Trades and Leveling]]:** the witchcraft system as a separate, parallel progression path (alongside farming) — with its own resource inputs (dark forest ingredients) and ritual outputs (spells) — parallels Wayfinder's multi-trade model where each Trade has its own gather and craft chain.
