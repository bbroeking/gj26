# Nioh 2

Action-RPG (2020, Team Ninja/Koei Tecmo) — a soulslike set in Sengoku Japan where a half-yokai protagonist masters three weapon stances, a rhythm-based Ki Pulse stamina system, and a transformative Yokai Shift power, atop one of the genre's deepest loot ladders.

## Design

- **Ki Pulse (stamina rhythm).** After attacking, the Ki (stamina) meter begins refilling; pressing a button during a small timing window "pulses" the refill, restoring Ki significantly faster. Missing the window means waiting the full recovery duration. This converts stamina management from passive waiting into an active rhythm game layered on top of combat — every combo ends with a micro-decision.
- **Flux and stance-switching.** Three stances (High, Mid, Low) each alter damage output, dodge type, guard effectiveness, and available skills. The "Flux" mechanic rewards switching stances during a Ki Pulse for bonus stamina — switching twice through an intermediate stance gives compounding bonuses. Expert play chains attacks → Ki Pulse → stance switch → new stance skills in a single fluid loop.
- **Yokai Shift.** Players transform into a yokai form fueled by the Amrita meter, gaining access to powerful attacks, a separate health bar during the form, and immunity to the Dark Realm's Ki drain. Shift timing is both an offensive cooldown and a defensive escape valve.
- **Soul Cores and the Dark Realm.** Purifying Soul Cores from defeated yokai grants passive abilities and unlocks yokai-specific attacks. The Dark Realm — a corrupted zone activated by certain enemies — drains Ki rapidly unless the player defeats the zone's source, adding spatial urgency to encounters.
- **Three-player co-op.** Up to three players can cooperate. "Benevolent Graves" allow summoning AI-controlled versions of other players' characters, providing a soft co-op option without requiring live partners.
- **Deep loot system.** Loot quality, stats, and set bonuses approach ARPG depth (comparable to [[Diablo II]]-style games). This is both a strength (enormous build variety) and a accessibility barrier — new players face a daunting equipment screen from the first hour.

## Implementation

- Engine: Team Ninja proprietary (Nioh engine, iterating on the first game's base).
- Metacritic: 83–86/100 across platforms; OpenCritic 97% recommendation rate.
- Sales: 2.5 million units by February 2022; Nioh franchise exceeded 8 million by May 2025.
- PS5/PC release: February 5, 2021 (as part of Nioh 2 Complete Edition).
- Won Japan Game Awards "Award for Excellence" and The Steam Awards "Best Game You Suck At" (2021).
- Nioh 3 announced; releases February 2026.

## Why it matters

Ki Pulse is one of the most innovative stamina-management designs in action gaming: it transforms a passive recovery stat into a skill-expressive rhythm mechanic, ensuring that mastering Nioh 2 is never just about memorizing enemy patterns but also about executing your own timing correctly under pressure. Stance-switching and the Flux mechanic mean the game has a skill ceiling well beyond pattern recognition. The Yokai Shift adds a third resource layer (alongside HP and Ki) that ties thematic identity (half-yokai protagonist) directly to a mechanical advantage. The three-player scaling and Benevolent Graves system are practical lessons in co-op accessibility: always give players a way to receive help that doesn't require live partners.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **Ki Pulse as rhythm layer.** Wayfinder's "combat-as-one-verb" design could borrow the rhythm-based recovery concept: a simple timing beat during skill recovery that rewards attentiveness without adding a full additional button. See [[Combat]].
- **Stance / tool-switching identity.** Nioh 2's three stances provide build identity within a single weapon type. In Wayfinder, different chart affixes could function analogously — shaping how a combat skill behaves based on run parameters. See [[Skills]], [[Items and Gear]].
- **Benevolent Graves for async co-op.** The AI-companion-from-other-players'-ghosts model is a lightweight multiplayer on-ramp worth considering for early Wayfinder sessions before live co-op is polished. See [[Multiplayer Co-op]].
