# Elden Ring

Open-world action-RPG (2022, FromSoftware/Bandai Namco) — the soulslike formula exploded into the Lands Between, a seamless open world with six legacy dungeons gating demigod bosses, whose Great Rune drops serve as the game's key progression currency.

## Design

- **Open world + legacy dungeon duality.** The Lands Between is a traversable open world (horse mount Torrent, no loading screens between regions), but the densest content — and all mandatory progression — lives inside six Legacy Dungeons (Stormveil Castle, Raya Lucaria Academy, etc.) that play almost identically to classic Souls entries: narrow corridors, interlocking loops, ambushes. The open world is connective tissue and optional enrichment; the dungeons are the game.
- **Great Rune system as boss trophy chain.** Each demigod boss drops a Great Rune — a corrupted shard of the Elden Ring. Runes must be activated at Divine Towers before they grant passive stat bonuses. This is a two-step "collect trophy → empower it" loop: defeating a boss is not enough, the player must also journey to the associated Tower. Only two Great Runes are required to trigger the endgame, but the game ships twelve. The mandatory floor is low; the completionist ceiling is high.
- **Sites of Grace = bonfires with fast-travel.** Every Site of Grace is both a checkpoint and a fast-travel node (from the start of the game), removing the early-game spatial constraint that made Dark Souls's world feel tightly physical. The tradeoff: open-world navigation needed the horse; hand-crafted interconnection was replaced by breadth.
- **Spirit Ashes: opt-in AI companion.** Summonable NPC fighters called Spirit Ashes give solo players a pseudo-co-op option mid-combat. They can be upgraded and swapped, functioning as an accessible difficulty lever without touching enemy stats.
- **Death penalty.** Runes (the game's Souls equivalent) drop at death; one retrieval attempt before permanent loss. The loop is identical to Dark Souls but less punishing in feel because the open world offers more income sources and the death location is always findable on the map.
- **800+ weapons, build depth.** Arguably the deepest build variety in the series: weapons, Ashes of War (special moves), incantations, sorceries, and talismans all combine freely. The Great Rune passive bonuses layer on top, tying boss kill order to build optimization.

## Implementation

- Engine: proprietary FromSoftware engine (Dantelion engine, same lineage as Sekiro).
- Metacritic: 96/100 (PS5), 94/100 (PC). Over 300 Game of the Year awards across publications.
- Sold 30 million copies by April 2025, making it one of the best-selling games ever. DLC "Shadow of the Erdtree" (June 2024) sold 10 million copies.
- Online systems carry forward the invasion/co-op soapstone model from Dark Souls: summon signs, red invasion items, and the Tarnished Furled Finger for cooperative play. The open world complicated the co-op boundary: summon range is per-zone, not per-distance.
- George R.R. Martin contributed worldbuilding lore (the history before the game's events); Miyazaki directed the present-tense narrative and level design. The collaboration was unusual and commercially significant.

## Why it matters

Elden Ring proved the soulslike formula is not bound to corridor design: meaningful challenge, boss-gated progression, and risk-reward death loops survive in open space provided the *legacy dungeon* concept remains intact as the high-density delivery vehicle. The Great Rune system is the cleanest published example of a "boss trophy unlocks power" chain: two tokens required, twelve optional, each with an activation ritual — a design that separates "defeating the boss" from "claiming the reward" and adds a second journey per trophy.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **Trophy chain architecture.** Wayfinder's boss-trophy-unlocks-deeper-den chain is structurally the same as Great Runes: each trophy is a progression gate rather than an inert collectible. The Great Rune model adds an "activation journey" on top of the kill — worth considering whether Wayfinder charters should require a second step (inscribing a new chart affix, visiting an NPC) to convert a trophy into its unlock. See [[Bosses]], [[Items and Gear]].
- **Legacy dungeon as the high-density core.** Wayfinder's chart-seeded dungeons are its legacy dungeons. The Elden Ring lesson is that the open/exploration layer can be lower-stakes and navigational, as long as the dungeon itself is tight and authored-feeling (even if procedurally seeded). See [[Camera and Game Feel]], [[Enemies]].
- **Spirit Ashes as solo-accessibility lever.** An opt-in AI ally that scales difficulty without touching numbers is a transferable pattern for Wayfinder's 2–4 player co-op: a single-player mode could offer a Spirit Ash equivalent without bifurcating the game's balance. See [[Multiplayer Co-op]], [[Combat]].
