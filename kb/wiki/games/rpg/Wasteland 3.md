# Wasteland 3

Squad-based post-apocalyptic CRPG (2020, inXile Entertainment) notable for being among the first isometric RPGs to offer full co-op campaign play for two players — a design that forced every system (skill checks, faction reputation, quest resolution) to function when the party is split across two independent human players.

## Design

- **Full two-player co-op throughout the campaign.** Both players control separate Ranger squads sharing a companion pool. They can travel together, split and tackle different objectives simultaneously, or converge for major fights. The critical mechanic: players can turn in the same quest to rival factions at the same time, getting double rewards or triggering mutually exclusive story consequences. The game is designed to accommodate asynchronous sessions — a player can continue solo when their partner logs off, and the partner rejoins the live save later.
- **Skill-check synergy across squads.** When a dialogue skill check triggers, the game evaluates the highest relevant skill across *the entire party* — including the co-op partner's squad. A partner with Weird Science handles the tech puzzle even if they are across the map. This design deliberately rewards building *complementary* teams rather than mirroring loadouts, and makes the co-op feel mechanically meaningful rather than cosmetic.
- **Faction reputation on a five-step scale.** Reputation tracks with Colorado's factions (Gippers, Hundred Families, Payasos, Marshals, et al.) from Hated through Loved, shaping quest access, random encounters, and store availability. Choosing to betray the mission's original patron (the Patriarch) is a live story option unlocked by reputation with opposing factions, making the reputation system the moral compass of the run.
- **Choice and consequence at quest and campaign scale.** The core ethical trap: players arrive as Arizona Rangers promised aid by the Patriarch in exchange for recovering his children — then discover the Patriarch's brutal governance. The game does not force a binary; players can complete the mission, rebel, or broker a third path. These choices cascade into multiple endings for Colorado, each faction, and individual companion storylines.
- **Vehicle traversal and environmental hazards.** A party vehicle (the Kodiak) serves as the world-map transport and provides light combat utility. Environmental hazards (blizzards, radiation zones) gate areas until skills or gear meet thresholds, providing exploration pacing akin to CRPG "traversal locks."

## Implementation

- **Unity engine.** Released August 28, 2020 for PC/console (Linux/macOS port December 2020). Metacritic 85/100 PC; nominated for Best RPG at The Game Awards 2020 and D.I.C.E. 2021 (both lost to FF7 Remake). Reached 2 million players by December 2021.
- **Co-op tech was a major production investment.** inXile had to implement synchronous session management, async save state, and split-squad mission tracking — novel for the CRPG genre at full campaign scope. The decision was influenced by player demand voiced during the Wasteland 3 Fig crowdfunding campaign.

## Why it matters

Wasteland 3 is the proof-of-concept that a story-driven, choice-heavy CRPG can accommodate two-player co-op *without* reducing the story to a co-op-safe skeleton. The skill-synergy-across-squads mechanic is the cleanest solution yet found to "how do two players meaningfully share a skill system" — you specialize, not duplicate, and the game rewards it. The asynchronous session model solves the scheduling problem for co-op RPGs without breaking campaign continuity.

## Relevance to Wayfinder

- **[[Multiplayer Co-op]]:** The skill-synergy-across-squads mechanic is directly applicable to Wayfinder's Trade system in co-op: an Earthcrafter and a Wildcraftter in party should contribute distinct skill-check passes, not race to have the same skill. The async session design (play solo when partner is offline, rejoin seamlessly) is the exact co-op pattern Wayfinder should aim for.
- **[[Trades and Leveling]]:** The "complementary loadouts rewarded" philosophy maps directly to Wayfinder co-op: players who bring different Trades to a chart run should unlock possibilities neither could alone. Chart affixes could flag which Trades unlock hidden cache rooms or bypass encounters.
- **[[Combat]]:** The squad-level tactical structure (specialized roles, cover, fast lethal encounters) is a useful reference for how Wayfinder's "combat as one verb" could feel: decisive, role-defined, and short — not the attrition-grinding of MMO trash pulls.
