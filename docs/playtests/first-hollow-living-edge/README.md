## Review

The room center remains open and immediately readable, while the perimeter now
has foreground banks, a darker rear foliage shoulder, varied stone/root/fern
rhythm, and room-owned landmarks. The compound profiles still lower and restore
as existing wall units under the continuous FATE camera.

The concept remains richer in bespoke foliage silhouettes and tonal depth. The
largest isolated residual gap is now Web grading: its green values are brighter
and flatter than native and the approved concept. That belongs to a focused
rendering checkpoint rather than more room systems.
