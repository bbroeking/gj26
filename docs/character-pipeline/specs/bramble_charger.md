# Bramble Charger

**Role:** Enemy (smaller, faster bramble-grown boar)

**One-liner:** Bristly leaf-shrouded boar with glowing amber eyes, two upturned tusks, and bramble plates strapped to its flanks.

## Visual key features
- Compact quadruped silhouette — leaner and more aggressive than Tusker Sow
- Body almost entirely buried under dense layered leaves (mid-green with darker undersides)
- Pale cream snout pushing forward from leaf-mass; flared nostrils
- Two upturned cream tusks; glowing amber/orange eyes
- Wood-plank shoulder/flank armor pieces strapped on with dark leather
- Stubby split-hoof legs visible below leaf skirt
- Leaf-tuft running spine to tail

## Palette
- Leaves (green): `#46582E`
- Leaves (highlight): `#7E933E`
- Snout (cream): `#D8C49A`
- Tusks (cream): `#EFE0B6`
- Eyes (glow amber): `#F0962A`
- Plank armor (brown): `#6E4D2E`
- Leather strap: `#3A2516`
- Hoof (dark): `#2E2218`

**Rig type:** quadruped (4 legs)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones as the dominant surface — coats almost the entire body (signature look); painted-stripes for plank seams; emissive flat color on eyes for glow.

**Notable challenges:** Leaf-cones must cover the body uniformly without burying the snout/tusk silhouette. Quadruped charge animation needs the head-low-shoulders-high pose to read in 1-2 keyframes.
