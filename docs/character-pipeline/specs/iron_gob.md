# Iron Gob

**Role:** Enemy (heavy-armored goblin brute)

**One-liner:** Squat green goblin in a riveted slate-blue plate suit, hauling a chunky stone-headed warhammer almost as tall as he is.

## Visual key features
- Chibi goblin proportions: huge head/helmet, short legs, broad shoulders
- Green skin face peering out from open-front helm; orange glowing eyes
- Pointed goblin ears poking through helmet slots
- Slate-blue riveted plate armor: helm, pauldrons, breastplate, greaves — orange rivets/accents
- Brown leather kilt-skirt under cuirass; bare green forearms and feet
- Two-handed grey stone hammer with wood haft and leather grip wrap

## Palette
- Skin (goblin green): `#7C9A5A`
- Armor (slate blue): `#3F5566`
- Rivets/accent (orange): `#D87330`
- Eyes (glow orange): `#FF9B3D`
- Kilt (leather brown): `#6B4A2E`
- Hammer head (stone grey): `#9A9A94`
- Hammer haft (wood): `#7A5536`
- Strap leather: `#4A2F1E`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid)

**Surface texture approach:** painted-stripes for armor plate edges and rivets — no extra geometry. None on skin.

**Notable challenges:** Hammer is roughly chest-height and weighty — two-handed idle pose must not clip the breastplate or kilt. Helmet eye-glow needs emissive material so it survives toonification.
