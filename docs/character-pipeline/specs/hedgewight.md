# Hedgewight

**Role:** Boss (mini-boss / lieutenant — bramble-grown bear-beast)

**One-liner:** Stocky four-legged bear-creature with a pale skull-face, body buried under a mound of leaves, planks, and small flowers.

## Visual key features
- Heavy quadruped silhouette: thick legs, low broad shoulders, bear-like head
- Pale cream skull-mask face with dark eye-pits and broad muzzle
- Massive leaf-and-plank "shell" mound covering back, shoulders, hips
- Small bright flower clusters (yellow, blue, white) dotting the leaf mass
- Wood-plank body armor strapped under leaves; warm brown bark legs
- Dark thick-stub claws on each foot, no visible tail

## Palette
- Skull-face (cream): `#E0D2B0`
- Skull shadow: `#A89880`
- Leaves (green): `#5A7236`
- Leaves (highlight): `#94AE48`
- Plank body (warm brown): `#7A5236`
- Plank shadow: `#3E2A1A`
- Flower (yellow): `#E8C44A`
- Flower (cool blue): `#4A6FA0`

**Rig type:** quadruped (4 legs)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones across the entire dorsal mound (signature look); leaf-cones in a smaller scale + a few quad flower-petal cones for the flowers; painted-stripes for plank seams and skull-mask shadow.

**Notable challenges:** Custom approach — flower clusters need a separate small-scale leaf-cone variant (color-tinted) so they read as petals against the green mass. Quadruped boss rig with heavier idle breathing animation than Tusker/Charger.
