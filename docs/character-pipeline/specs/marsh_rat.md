# Marsh Rat

**Role:** Enemy (small painted scaly quadruped)

**One-liner:** Squat four-legged marsh creature plated in slate-blue scales, with a fan of red and orange leaf-petals running from snout-ridge to tail.

## Visual key features
- Low quadruped silhouette: barrel body, four short stubby legs, long tapered scaled tail
- Pale cream face/snout with single big black eye glint
- Dorsal crest of upright leaf-petals (red, orange, deep coral) running spine-line
- Body fully clad in overlapping slate-blue diamond scales with green moss patches
- Pink tongue lolls from snout
- Tiny pointed ears tucked behind petal crest

## Palette
- Scales (slate blue): `#3D556A`
- Face (cream): `#E2D4B6`
- Petals (red): `#B23A33`
- Petals (orange): `#D8772E`
- Moss accent: `#6E833A`
- Belly/feet (warm tan): `#A88862`
- Tongue (pink): `#D87A86`
- Eye black: `#1A1A1E`

**Rig type:** quadruped (4 legs + tail)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones for the dorsal petal crest (small flat cones, two rows); painted-stripes on body for scale tiling rather than per-scale geometry.

**Notable challenges:** Custom approach — petal crest is signature silhouette and must read in profile, but cones must lay back along the spine, not stick straight up like a stegosaurus. Quadruped walk cycle, not the standard biped rig.
