# Skitterling

**Role:** Enemy (small leaf-clad sprite / lesser bramble spirit)

**One-liner:** Pale-faced child-sized woodling fully shrouded in overlapping leaves, clutching a crooked twig staff, eyes two black hollows.

## Visual key features
- Small chibi humanoid silhouette, knee-high to player
- Hooded cowl woven entirely from leaves (green with rust-orange tips)
- Pale linen-bag face, two big featureless black holes for eyes
- Layered leaf-skirt over a weathered tan tunic; leather harness strap across chest
- Crooked thin twig staff, taller than the creature, in right hand
- Small wooden satchel on opposite hip

## Palette
- Face (pale linen): `#E4D8C2`
- Leaf (mid green): `#5E7A36`
- Leaf (rust tip): `#B0612A`
- Tunic (tan): `#A88A5C`
- Strap leather: `#4A3220`
- Staff (wood): `#6B4A2C`
- Satchel: `#8C6A3F`
- Eye holes: `#0B0B10`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones layered as the entire hood + cape + skirt (this is the signature look); painted-stripes for tunic seams.

**Notable challenges:** Custom approach — leaf-cones do all the heavy lifting visually, so layering needs to be dense without exploding tri count. Staff is taller than the body, so idle pose has to angle it back to keep silhouette readable.
