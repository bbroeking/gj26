# Quill

**Role:** NPC (young forager / apprentice cartographer)

**One-liner:** Round-faced chibi child with huge curly auburn hair, mossy-green hooded cape, and a logged-up forager's pack bristling with leaves.

## Visual key features
- Big round head, tiny body — most extreme chibi ratio in the cast
- Voluminous curly auburn hair with leaf sprigs tucked in
- Mossy green half-cape with hood thrown back
- Tan/beige tunic dress, cinched with brown belt and small pouch
- Forager's pack: wooden frame with strapped log, leaves, herb bundles
- Tiny brown ankle boots; standing on a small grass tuft base

## Palette
- Skin: `#F2C9A7`
- Hair (auburn): `#B8662E`
- Hair highlight: `#D8954A`
- Cape (moss green): `#5A6E3A`
- Tunic (beige): `#C9B68A`
- Belt/boots (brown): `#5A3826`
- Leaves (fresh green): `#6E8C36`
- Pack wood: `#8C6A3F`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid)

**Surface texture approach:** displaced-mass for the hair (single cap mesh with vertex jitter to read as curls); leaf-cones for the leaf sprigs in hair and pack.

**Notable challenges:** Hair volume is half the silhouette — must not eat the face plane. Pack contents (logs, leaves) need to be a single welded prop, not loose floaters that pop through walk cycle.
