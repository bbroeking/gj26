# Tusker Sow

**Role:** Enemy (large bramble-grown wild boar matriarch)

**One-liner:** Massive armored boar overgrown with planks, leaves, and bramble, with curling cream tusks and a green dorsal crest.

## Visual key features
- Heavy quadruped silhouette: deep barrel chest, short stout legs, low slung head
- Stylized "wood-plank" body construction — looks built from bound timber slats with leather strapping
- Dense moss-green leaf clumps along spine, shoulders, and rump
- Two large curling cream-yellow tusks jutting up and out from lower jaw
- Small dark eyes, broad flat snout
- Hoofed stubby legs (no claws), tail not prominent

## Palette
- Plank body (warm brown): `#7A5A3A`
- Plank shadow: `#4A3220`
- Leaf clumps (green): `#5C7438`
- Leaf highlight (yellow-green): `#9AAE48`
- Tusks (cream): `#E8DCB2`
- Strap leather (dark): `#3A2616`
- Snout (warm tan): `#A88058`
- Eyes: `#1A1612`

**Rig type:** quadruped (4 legs)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones for the dorsal/shoulder leaf clumps; painted-stripes for the plank seams on body — keeps tri count down vs. modeling each plank.

**Notable challenges:** Quadruped walk + charge animation. Tusks are silhouette-critical and must clear the snout from every camera angle. Leaf-clumps need to mass into 3-4 readable shapes, not scatter as noise.
