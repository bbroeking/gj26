# Hedgemother

**Role:** Boss (matriarch bramble-spirit, hedgerow given form)

**One-liner:** Hulking squat boss-creature crowned with twisted antler-branches and red berries, body a tangled mass of leaves, roots, and bark over glowing yellow eyes.

## Visual key features
- Wide, squat boss silhouette — broader than tall, crouched on knuckle-arms
- Crown of crooked branching antlers studded with bright red berry clusters
- Two pairs of large glowing yellow eyes (or one pair plus inner light) on a dark bark-face
- Body a layered tangle of moss-green leaves over deep brown root/bark torso
- Long heavy bark-knuckle arms reaching the ground; small stubby legs
- Vine "beard" or root-tendrils hanging from chin
- Berry clusters echo across shoulders and back

## Palette
- Bark (dark): `#3A281C`
- Bark (warm mid): `#6E4A2E`
- Leaves (deep green): `#3E5128`
- Leaves (highlight): `#7C9A3E`
- Berries (red): `#B83830`
- Eye glow (yellow): `#F2C033`
- Antler (pale wood): `#B89868`
- Vine/root accent: `#5A4A2E`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid, knuckle-walker proportions — long arms, short legs)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones for body shoulders, crown filler, and back — the bramble-mantle is the signature read; strand-cones for the root-tendril chin beard and antler tines; painted-stripes for bark grain on torso/arms.

**Notable challenges:** Custom approach — combines two surface-texture systems (leaf-cones AND strand-cones) on one rig. Glow eyes require emissive material that survives toonification. Antler crown must read clean at silhouette and not noise out against leaves behind it.
