# Sir Withering

**Role:** NPC (chibi gnome-knight with falcon companion)

**One-liner:** Aged, stubby-statured knight in patched layered cloak and tabard, with a green-grey falcon perched on his outstretched gauntlet.

## Visual key features
- Stout chibi proportions: oversized head, tiny legs, large brown boots
- Frizzy white hair + full forked white beard, ruddy bulbous nose
- Layered garb: deep teal hooded cloak over a cream tabard with red diamond patch and brass studs
- Belt with two small leather pouches at hip
- Falcon companion (separate small mesh) — slate-blue back, cream chest, hooked yellow beak
- Posture: standing square, one arm out at chest height as bird perch

## Palette
- Skin: `#E2A98A`
- Hair/beard: `#E8E4DC`
- Cloak (teal): `#3F5D6A`
- Tabard (cream): `#D9CDB2`
- Patch (red): `#A8423B`
- Boots (brown): `#5A3A28`
- Falcon body: `#5C7480`
- Falcon beak/feet: `#D9A53C`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid). Falcon is a separate small biped/winged prop.

**Surface texture approach:** strand-cones for the forked beard tips and side-tufts of hair; painted-stripes for cloak patches and tabard markings.

**Notable challenges:** Falcon must parent to the right gauntlet bone so it rides through walk cycle without sliding. Beard fork has to read at silhouette without poking through chest cloak.
