# Bramble Archer

**Role:** Enemy (ranged bramble-cult skirmisher)

**One-liner:** Hooded chibi creature in tattered green leaf-cloak, drawing a curved twig bow, face hidden behind a pale beak-mask.

## Visual key features
- Small biped silhouette, slightly hunched into a draw stance
- Pale dome-hood/mask with dark twin eye-slits and pointed beak; reads as a ghostly owl-skull
- Layered tattered cloak in green leaves with rusty-orange neck wrap
- Thigh-length green tunic, brown leather greaves and boots
- Quiver of mismatched feather-fletched arrows over back/hip
- Curved organic-twig recurve bow drawn at chest height; vine bowstring

## Palette
- Mask (pale bone): `#E0D2B6`
- Mask shadow: `#9A8C72`
- Cloak (deep green): `#48603A`
- Leaf highlight: `#7C9A48`
- Neck wrap (rust): `#B25E2C`
- Tunic (mossy): `#6E7E48`
- Leather (brown): `#5A3D26`
- Bow/arrows (wood): `#7A5536`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid)

**Surface texture approach:** leaf-cones layered along cloak shoulders and skirt edges (key silhouette element); painted-stripes for mask eye-slits and arrow fletching colors.

**Notable challenges:** Drawn-bow pose is the signature read — both hands must hold props (bow + arrow nock), so the rig needs proper hand parenting. Beak-mask must not clip into the high collar leaves.
