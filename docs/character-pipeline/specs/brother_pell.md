# Brother Pell

**Role:** NPC (mendicant scribe / wandering brother)

**One-liner:** Stout chibi monk in cream robe and travel-rolls, holding an open scroll, with a thick brown beard and bald-crowned dome.

## Visual key features
- Wide barrel torso, short legs, large flat brown boots
- Bald crown ringed by tonsure of brown hair; massive square brown beard with grey streaks
- Cream/off-white belted robe with cracked dark trim, leather belt with brass medallion clasp
- Bedroll/scroll-roll slung diagonally across back (slate-blue cloth wrap)
- Open scroll held in left hand, displayed at chest
- Small belt-pouch on right hip

## Palette
- Skin: `#E5B391`
- Beard (brown): `#5C3E2A`
- Beard highlight (grey): `#A89884`
- Robe (cream): `#E4D8B8`
- Robe trim (dark): `#3A3026`
- Bedroll (slate): `#4A5C68`
- Belt/pouch (leather): `#6B4530`
- Medallion (brass): `#C49A3F`

**Rig type:** biped (humanoid)

**Surface texture approach:** strand-cones for the squared-off beard tips; painted-stripes for robe trim, belt detail, and the lined-text scroll.

**Notable challenges:** Held scroll prop should be a flat plane with painted text — needs to parent to the left hand without clipping through belly. Beard volume must not crash into the medallion.
