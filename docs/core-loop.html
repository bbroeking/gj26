The Core Loop

What you do every 30 seconds, every session, and every week — and why it's worth doing.

The spine (locked — ADR 0003)

You are a resident of Bramblewood. Gather, cook, craft, and chart are the heart of the game; combat is one verb among many. Every loop below returns to skilling — combat is punctuation, not the point.

The three nested loops

every ~30 seconds

The gather beat

  1. Walk to a node (herb, fish, tree, vein)
  2. Channel-gather — hold, progress bar, an animation
  3. Resource + XP burst + occasional rare (luck pop)
  4. Move to the next node — or fight an enemy that wanders in
every ~20–30 min session

The craft cycle

  1. Gather a bag of raw materials in the world
  2. Return to the hearth / station
  3. Cook / smelt / process into useful goods
  4. Consume, equip, or bank
  5. Push a skill toward its next milestone
every few sessions / week

The chart loop

  1. Gather inscribing materials
  2. Cartography — inscribe a new chart
  3. The chart reveals / spawns a new dungeon
  4. It unlocks a higher resource tier or area
  5. …which deepens the gather + craft loops above

Why it's fun

Earned mastery

The meaningful number — New World skill depth + luck + cartography milestones. The OSRS "number-go-up" that takes real time and means real status.

Discovery

Cartography literally makes new places. The meta-loop is genuinely novel — not a genre copy. Charts are the engine of "what's over there?"

Belonging + slow time

Named Bramblewood neighbors, meditative gathering, low pressure. The world has its own pace and you're a guest in it.

Variable-ratio surprise

The luck / rare-drop pop (spec 37) gives every node a chance to spit out something special — the slot-machine beat under the meditation.

How it feels

Meditative, slow, cozy. The channel-gather sets the pace — deliberate, not twitchy. Combat is occasional punctuation, not constant threat; you can put the controller down between beats. The dopamine comes from XP bursts and level-up jingles, not from dodging death. On the feel framework it scores 2/2 on earned mastery, slow time, and discovery — the three feelings this game is built to evoke.

How we achieve it — the first slice

Vertical-slice-first. We don't theorize the loop — we build the smallest complete version in Godot and feel it. The first slice is gather → cook → sustain → fight:

  1. Add the spec-37 channel-gather to one forageable + one fishing spot
  2. Wire the gathered item into the existing hearth cook flow
  3. Confirm cooked food heals
  4. Confirm a short encounter consumes it
  5. Award Wilds + Cook XP on the actions

Godot already has the hearth, combat, and inventory — the only net-new is the channel-gather. Then: playtest and ask "is this fun?" Cartography (the differentiator) is slice 2, layered on a base loop that already feels right.
