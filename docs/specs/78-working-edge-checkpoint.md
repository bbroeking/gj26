# Spec 78 — Working Edge checkpoint

## Player-visible problem

Gathered Arrival puts the northern cottage, Chartmaker tower, and smithy into
the first controllable frame, but only as clipped, widely separated border
objects. Their work props and neighbors sit apart from their buildings, so the
yard still reads as a large field with three destinations rather than Mara's
small inhabited working home. The approved Arrival concept uses the same basic
grammar—three north landmarks around an open crossing—but gathers them into a
continuous architectural edge.

## Promise

The existing cottage, Chartmaker tower, and smithy form one readable northern
working edge around Mara's yard. Their doors, bases, and owned work clusters
enter the settled arrival frame while the ranger-to-Mara route and central
crossing remain visibly and physically open.

## Scope

- Re-stage only the three existing northern landmark positions and the support
  pieces that already belong to them.
- Keep each neighbor and station attached to its established landmark through
  explicit relative placement rather than unrelated world coordinates.
- Rebuild the same path and dressing projection from the accepted layout.
- Compare matched native and Web 1280×720 captures with Gathered Arrival and
  the approved Arrival concept.
- Add a focused layout contract and keep the full checkpoint gate green.

## Boundaries

- Add no building, NPC, station, interaction, path destination, progression
  landmark, currency, content track, or new asset.
- Do not change the accepted Town camera profile, tonal grade, player/Mara
  positions, central plaza, Waystone, Chart table, controls, audio, quests,
  collision ownership, save schema, or First Road presentation.
- Do not overlap the Waystone, Chart table, practice space, Mara, or the open
  arrival route with a landmark collider or support prop.
- Later campaign projections must continue deriving from their owning landmark
  and remain deterministic.

## Acceptance

- At 1280×720, the settled fresh arrival shows the doors or readable lower
  facades of all three northern landmarks, not only clipped bases.
- Cottage, tower, and smithy read as one supporting band around the social
  route; Mara and the ranger remain the primary silhouettes.
- The direct spawn → Mara route, central crossing, Waystone approach, Chart
  table approach, and practice space retain safe clearance.
- Native Forward+ and Web Compatibility agree on composition; the approved
  concept comparison shows a material gain over Gathered Arrival.
- Focused tests, canonical assertions, save roundtrip, release-resource audit,
  exact export, rendered browser journey, diagnostic ledger, and release chain
  all pass before shipping.
