# Spec 77 — Gathered Arrival checkpoint

## Player-visible problem

Shaded Home restores the yard's tonal hierarchy, but the first playable camera
still centers a broad empty lawn. Mara sits against the top edge and the three
northern landmarks fall mostly outside the frame. Beside the approved Arrival
concept, Bramblewood reads as a test field with scattered stations rather than
a small working home gathered around a new ranger.

## Promise

The first controllable Town frame gathers the ranger, Mara, the Waystone, the
Chart table, and at least the readable bases of the northern landmark cluster
into one coherent home view. The player remains clearly anchored in the lower
half of the frame, with the road to Mara unobstructed.

## Scope

- Give Town one authored gameplay-camera profile using the established FATE
  camera rig and its existing orbit and zoom controls.
- Carry that profile through the first-arrival vignette's return frame and the
  homecoming pullback.
- Compare native and Web captures at 1280×720 with the approved Arrival concept.
- Add a focused contract for the Town profile and keep the canonical gate green.

## Boundaries

- Do not move the player, Mara, stations, paths, buildings, trees, fences, or
  progression landmarks.
- Do not change the global First Road camera feel, player controls, collision,
  prompts, quests, audio, progression, saves, or network authority.
- Do not reproduce the concept by adding decorative geometry in this checkpoint.
- Do not remove player control of orbit or zoom after the arrival beat.

## Acceptance

- The settled first playable frame shows a materially stronger north-yard
  composition than Shaded Home: Mara is away from the top edge and the landmark
  cluster contributes visible architectural framing.
- The ranger remains readable in the lower half, the direct route to Mara stays
  open, and no HUD or quest parchment is clipped.
- Native Forward+ and Web Compatibility agree on the composition at 1280×720.
- The exact exported build completes title → Town → chart table → Codex → First
  Road → return with no warning or error diagnostics.
- Canonical assertions, resource audit, save roundtrip, changelog, roadmap,
  devlog, version history, deployment, and companion Field Journal all agree.
