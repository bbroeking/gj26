# 75 — Creature identity checkpoint

> **Outcome:** every creature met along the remembered roads is recognizable
> before it lands a hit, keeps a distinct combat role after its opening tell,
> and has one coherent Field Journal record.

## Contract

- Keep the current single-player campaign, procedural Chart layouts, encounter
  counts, rewards, progression, and production FATE camera.
- Give every common creature a readable ranged opening with a kind-owned color,
  speed, damage, and written battle sign. The opening creates room to react; it
  does not replace the creature's melee, kiting, support, bruiser, or pursuit
  identity once distance closes.
- Keep named-creature signatures primary. The Hedgemother family, Barrow Jarl,
  and Hearth Giant may weave a periodic kind-colored cast between their
  authored attacks. The Burrow Boar keeps its charge/stomp language, the Wolf
  Alpha keeps its chained lunges, and the Knot-Eater keeps its binding ritual;
  those three do not receive a generic projectile cadence.
- Telegraph and projectile color must agree. Aim lines remain visible for at
  least 0.6 seconds and projectiles remain dodgeable at production movement
  speed.
- Add a Creature Codex to the Pause folio. It lists every production common and
  named creature, separates the two families, gives Nature/Roads/Battle sign
  copy in Bramblewood's voice, supports keyboard/controller focus, and closes
  back to the still-paused folio one layer at a time.
- Replace the pale crypt-era treasure chest with the authored Wayfinder chest.
  Its prompt, scanner reach, solid footprint, and two-roll treasure behavior
  must match the visible prop. Gilded roads add one bonus chest beyond the
  ordinary treasure-room chest.
- Do not add creatures, encounters, campaign rewards, Trades, Skills, or a new
  progression system in this checkpoint.

## Acceptance

1. The Codex covers every production `ENEMY_KINDS` and `BOSS_KINDS` id exactly
   once; every page has non-empty Nature, Roads, Battle sign, and lore copy.
2. Pause → Creature Codex opens one nested folio at 1280×720, assigns focus,
   changes the visible detail page through a semantic Button, and closes back
   to Pause without resuming the world.
3. Every common creature owns an explicit projectile profile. At least six
   visibly separated color families survive the production spawn path, and
   telegraph/projectile materials use the same kind color.
4. Common-creature signatures remain intact: ghosts and Bog Wisps kite,
   Wardens heal, Barrow Brutes use the heavy floor ring, and the remaining
   pursuers close for their per-kind melee rhythm after the opening.
5. Named-creature cadence is opt-in. Boar, Wolf, and Knot-Eater have no generic
   projectile cadence; the remaining participating bosses keep their existing
   authored phase and encounter mechanics.
6. The Wayfinder chest loads in a production room, has a solid footprint inside
   its scanner reach, presents its prompt above the lid, opens once, and still
   yields two treasure rolls. Gilded generation adds exactly one bonus chest.
7. Representative native encounters from First Road, Green Hollow, Mire,
   Summit, Pale Oath, Ashen Bough, and The Unwritten Road demonstrate distinct
   silhouettes and fair tells at the production camera.
8. The full canonical native gate, boot compile, resource audit, and exported
   Web title → town → generated road → return receipt pass with an empty
   diagnostic ledger. Native/Web evidence is retained with the checkpoint.
