# 70 — Open Canopy

> Tighten the First Road's presentation-only forest shoulder so the continuous
> FATE camera keeps a generous readable stage, without reopening the black void
> or changing procedural rooms, wall truth, or cutaway ownership.

## Player-visible problem

The `0.1.9-soft-ground` replay confirms that the warm clearing now holds, but
the nearest two-tier Deep Wood masses project as oversized foreground blobs.
They crowd the lower playfield even when the ranger remains technically
visible. The approved First Hollow concept uses lush boundaries as a frame; it
does not let them consume the combat stage.

## Bounded change

- Keep both seeded rear foliage tiers on every First Road boundary profile.
- Keep their depth, dark palette, presentation-only status, and ownership under
  the cutaway-controlled `WallMesh`.
- Reduce their radius, vertical height, and lateral scale; settle them lower so
  they read as forest shoulder rather than a second wall canopy.
- Widen the existing screen-space cutaway stage so neighboring foreground wall
  crowns lower with the directly occluding wall instead of forming two tall
  shoulders beside the ranger.
- Change no grid, room archetype, corridor, collision, navigation, encounter,
  camera, controls, audio, or later-biome presentation.

## Acceptance

1. Native and Web play show more open ground around the ranger while retaining
   continuous forest beyond every First Road wall.
2. Every living wall still owns exactly two seeded, presentation-only depth
   tiers under its `WallMesh`.
3. The far tier still reaches at least 1.35 m behind the playable boundary.
4. Each depth sphere stays within the new compact radius/height/lateral-scale
   budget.
5. The established opaque cutaway protects a 510×340 px combat stage while
   retaining collision and the low solid boundary.
6. Room grammar, gameplay, and release gates pass.

## No-go boundaries

- No camera-angle or camera-follow change.
- No authored replacement for the procedural Chart graph.
- No removal of the two-tier depth read or reintroduction of black gaps.
- No new shader, texture, mesh system, collision, or gameplay authority.
