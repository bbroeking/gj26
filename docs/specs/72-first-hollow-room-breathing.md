# 72 — First Hollow room breathing

> **Outcome:** randomized First Road archetypes keep their recognizable forest
> landmarks, but supporting props frame clearings instead of barricading them.

## Player-visible contract

- The procedural Chart graph, seeded archetypes, room silhouettes, continuous
  FATE camera, natural cutaway walls, encounters, controls, and rewards remain
  unchanged.
- Full-size stump, fairy-ring, bramble-arch, and fallen-log landmarks retain
  each archetype's identity.
- Ferns, toadstools, and mossy boulders read as supporting rhythm rather than
  two-tile-wide landmarks.
- Every corridor mouth opens onto a three-wide, two-deep landing free of
  satellite dressing.
- Focal and typed-room fallback behavior remains reliable, while satellites may
  be omitted in a compact room rather than pinching its travel lane.
- The change is limited to the First Road composition kit.

## Acceptance

1. Same-seed decor, scale metadata, and approach clearance reproduce exactly.
2. A six-seed sample retains supporting dressing, keeps landmarks full-size,
   and places no First Road satellite inside a protected landing.
3. The rendered support meshes use the authored scale while existing collision
   truth, navigation, and interaction ownership remain unchanged.
4. Native play shows clearer entrances and more open compositions beside the
   approved First Hollow concept without weakening archetype identity.
5. The canonical and retained native gates remain green; the exact Web export
   passes resource audit and title → town → First Road → return with no browser
   diagnostics.
