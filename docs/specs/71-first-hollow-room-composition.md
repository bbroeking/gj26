# 71 — First Hollow room composition

> **Outcome:** randomized First Road archetypes read as different Bramblewood
> clearings in play, not merely different floor masks dressed by crypt-era
> theme logic.

## Player-visible contract

- The Chart graph, room count, connections, role assignment, and seed replay
  remain procedural.
- Lantern Landing, Round Glade, Crooked Bower, and Long Clearing each own one
  recognizable forest anchor and a restrained supporting rhythm.
- Combat anchors remain outside the protected movement-and-aiming aperture.
- Treasure, Shrine, Hearth, and boss focal contracts remain unchanged.
- The continuous FATE camera and natural cutaway wall behavior remain unchanged.
- The proof is limited to the First Road; later biome dressing is unaffected.

## Composition kit

| Archetype | Anchor | Supporting rhythm |
|---|---|---|
| Lantern Landing | hollow stump | ferns and toadstools at the arrival edge |
| Round Glade | fairy ring | clustered toadstools and mossy boulders |
| Crooked Bower | bramble arch | clustered toadstools and edge ferns |
| Long Clearing | fallen log | sparse boulders and edge ferns |

These are placement vocabularies, not fixed maps. Seeded room shapes, anchor
positions, supporting counts, route graph, encounters, and landmark placement
continue to vary.

## Acceptance

1. The same seed reproduces room archetypes, themes, and decor.
2. A multi-seed sample exercises all four composition kits.
3. Every arrival/combat archetype uses its matching kit and realizes its focal.
4. No combat decor enters the protected aperture or corridor mouths.
5. Later biomes do not inherit the kit.
6. Native and Web play show recognizably different room anchors without camera
   or route regression.
7. The full canonical and retained gates remain green.
