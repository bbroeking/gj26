# 74 — First Hollow readable passages

> **Outcome:** every procedural First Road connection reads as an inviting
> opening between rooms, while living walls remain natural, solid, and locally
> yielding under the continuous FATE camera.

## Contract

- Keep the procedural Chart graph, randomized authored room archetypes,
  corridor widths, collision, navigation, encounters, controls, rewards, and
  continuous camera.
- Keep dense Bramblewood beyond the playable boundary and the established
  layered living-wall grammar away from room connections.
- At each real room/corridor connection, taper only the visible wall shoulders
  into a lower, quieter, concave frame so the floor route remains obvious from
  the room and during camera rotation.
- Keep the wall's physical boundary and projectile truth aligned with the
  generated grid; this checkpoint changes presentation, not connectivity.
- Preserve the existing local screen-space cutaway and prove the new passage
  profile participates in it as one wall unit.
- Do not touch the in-progress user-owned Chest or creature/codex work.

## Acceptance

1. Same-seed room connections and wall cells reproduce exactly.
2. Every First Road room connection receives a deterministic passage profile;
   ordinary perimeter cells retain the accepted living-edge profiles.
3. Passage shoulders are visibly lower and less bulky than ordinary boundary
   crowns without exposing black void or removing physical wall truth.
4. The player, immediate combat lane, and connected floor remain legible at
   the default angle and through continuous camera yaw.
5. Later biomes retain their existing wall presentation.
6. Native and Web comparison with the approved First Hollow concept shows a
   readable framed opening; full release gates remain green.
