A Codex of

Bramblewood — Ability Codex

The ranger's four skills, the four ailments she leaves behind, the golden-hearted foes who carry burdens, and the Hedgemother herself — bound in one parchment.

Concept art and captured animations land in docs/skills/concept-art/ and docs/skills/captures/ respectively. Slots below are placeholders.

The Four Skills

Bound to keys 1 through 4. Slot 1 also fires on F. Cost is paid in Focus (a single regenerating pool, max 50); cooldowns 2–4 scale with the of Swiftness affix.

BasicShot

Slot 1 · F

cost 0 · cd 0.28s · damage 1.0× · gold visual

The default arrow. Free, snappy, on its own cooldown. The muscle-memory shot that fires whenever your finger drifts toward F. Cooldown scales with the fire_rate affix.

PowerShot

Slot 2

cost 20 · cd 1.5s · damage 2.5× · burn on hit

A heavy, slow arrow at two-and-a-half times BasicShot's damage. on hit · burn The struck target catches a slow fire — 1 damage per 0.5 seconds for 3 seconds (6 total).

MultiShot

Slot 3

cost 25 · cd 2.0s · 3 arrows · ±20° cone · snared on each hit

Three arrows in a wide forty-degree spread, each carrying half the base damage (the three together hit for 1.5×). on hit · snared Anything they touch crawls at half speed for 1.5 seconds. The kite tool — keep the pack at arm's length while the snare holds.

Bramble Snare

Slot 4

cost 30 · cd 4.0s · 2.5m AoE · root for 2s

A circle of emerald vines erupts at four metres ahead, rooting every foe in a two-and-a-half metre radius in place. on hit · root No damage — pure control. Two seconds is a long time to line up the next shot.

The Four Ailments

What the ranger's arrows leave behind. The Hedgemother resists Burn and Bleed by half; she is unyielding against Snared and Root.

Burn

DoT · fire

3s duration · 0.5s ticks · 1 dmg/tick · 6 total

Orange flame above the head, soft orange tint pulse on each tick. Cast by PowerShot and by Sunlit elites in melee.

Bleed

DoT · physical

4s duration · 1s ticks · ~25% of crit dmg total

Red droplet particles above the head, soft red tint pulse. Applied passively whenever any of the ranger's arrows score a crit — never to the ranger by enemies. The reward for the crit roll.

Snared

soft CC · 50% slow

1.5s duration · no DoT · slow factor 0.5

Green vine particles at the feet. Applied by MultiShot hits. The pack kite: a three-arrow fan can snare three foes at once while you reposition.

Root

hard CC · immobilize

2s duration · no DoT · velocity zero

Emerald disc on the floor, vines wrapping the foe's feet. Cast by Bramble Snare in a 2.5m AoE. Knockback still applies — you can punt a rooted enemy around.

Elite Modifiers

Roughly one in eight foes carries a golden burden. Always with a retinue of two or three of their own kind. Visually unambiguous from the moment they spawn — golden tint, larger silhouette, slow-spinning ring of motes at the feet.

Brambled

on death

+25% HP · +25% scale

When she falls, brambles burst from where she stood — a 2.5m circle, every foe within bleeds for 4 seconds. The death pop is the danger; getting close to the Brambled without clearing her retinue can chain.

on death · bleed nova

Swift

passive

no HP boost · +50% move · +20% attack speed · +15% scale

The one elite that doesn't get harder to kill, only harder to catch. Closes the gap before you can finish a MultiShot animation. Save your Bramble Snare for her — a slowed Swift becomes a normal foe.

Sunlit

on attack

+50% HP · +25% scale

Every melee swing pulses warmth — a Burn lands on whoever she touches, 3 seconds, 1 damage per half-second. The player feels it as 6 damage over time, plus the burning suffix appears in the HP bar.

on attack · burn pulse

Briarbound

passive

+30% HP · +15% scale · 4s CC-immune after first snare

Your Bramble Snare lands once. After that, four seconds of immunity to Root and Snared — she walks through your AoE untouched. Plan: the snare gets one window. Land your damage in it.

The Hedgemother

The crypt's one boss. Three phases by HP, telegraphed sweeping and stomping attacks. Immune to Root and Snared (Unyielding); takes Burn and Bleed at half duration.

The Hedgemother

Boss · Unyielding

60 HP · 3 phases at 100% / 66% / 33%

Phase 1: the long, slow telegraph. A green ground decal widens; if you're inside, you're hit. Phase 2: tighter telegraph, faster cooldown. Phase 3: barely any wind-up, constant aggression. The arena is sealed on her aggro; you don't leave until one of you falls.
