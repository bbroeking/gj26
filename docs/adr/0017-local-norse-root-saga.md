# ADR 0017 — A local Norse-inspired Root Saga may extend below Bramblewood

**Status:** Accepted direction (2026-07-17)

**Amends:** the “no multi-page mythology,” “doesn't escalate,” and “the
Hedgemother is the largest threat” constraints in `docs/WORLD_BIBLE.md` and
`docs/WORLD_LORE.md` for the full-game campaign. The warm voice, neighbor-scale
emotional focus, and no-grimdark rules remain binding.

## Context

The locked Bramblewood canon was written to protect a compact demo: one village,
small problems, and the Hedgemother as its highest threat. The full-game
direction now asks for an end-to-end story built from Norse mythology, delivered
through random charts and a chain of crafted boss maps.

Simply adding an apocalyptic pantheon would destroy the setting. Refusing any
story beyond the Hedgemother would ignore the new direction. The two scopes need
a precise seam.

## Decision

The full campaign may descend into the **rootroads beneath the Wolds**, whose
story echoes Yggdrasil, the Norns, draugr, jötnar, the root-gnawing creature,
and destruction followed by renewal.

The translation stays original and local:

- Old Mother Root is a living network under one enclosed valley, not a menu of
  nine explorable realms.
- The Three Mendwives echo fate-tenders but are not a renamed Norse pantheon.
- The Oathbound, Hearth Giant, and Knot-Eater are place-bound beings with
  understandable obligations, not an evil race or escalating demon army.
- The Ragged Winter threatens Bramblewood's remembered roads and relationships,
  not the universe.
- The climax repairs a cycle by inscribing a new road. The player does not kill
  a necessary creature or prevent the end of all creation.

The Hedgemother remains the largest and most important **bramble-being**, the
apex of the current Bramblewood chapter, and the first sincere mythic encounter.
Later bosses are not “Worse Brambles”; they live in older rootroad contexts and
are overcome by understanding their rules as well as fighting them.

## Story-delivery rule

Mythology is optional reward, never a prerequisite lore lecture. Random charts
carry one short clue, landmark, or repeated symbol. Authored Boss Charts carry
the irreversible character beats. Player-visible prose remains brief enough to
fit a dialog panel or item description.

## Consequences

- `WORLD_BIBLE.md` still governs voice, names, warmth, humor, and content limits
  everywhere this ADR does not explicitly amend it.
- The current Summit ending becomes a midpoint revelation in the full game.
- New biomes are conditions of the same root system—pale, flooded, ember-scarred,
  forgotten—not literal copies of Norse realms.
- Research and concept art should use primary mythic motifs, then translate them
  through Bramblewood rather than reproduce popular-media designs.
- If a chapter can only be pitched as “a bigger evil appears,” it fails this ADR.
