Design Spine

What a complete game design needs, ground-up — and where Bramblewood stands on each. The missing layers are the authoring queue.

The premise

A game, from first principles, is: a fantasy, realized as a core loop, expressed through verbs & systems, structured by progression & economy, situated in a world, challenged by encounters, motivated by narrative, sustained by retention, taught by onboarding, and made to feel good by polish. Each layer below is one of those. gj26 is strong on philosophy, pipeline, and a few vertical systems — the gaps are the horizontal spine that ties them into a game.

present authored for gj26 partial pieces exist, no spine missing only generic playbooks / other-game refs
